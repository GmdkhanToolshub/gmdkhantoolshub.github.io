Learning that fits into your day

Learn & Grow is for students, job seekers, professionals and anyone who enjoys learning something new. Every activity is short: a round of ten quiz questions takes about five minutes, a vocabulary session can be done in a coffee break, and the daily thought takes less than a minute to read.

The English practice is written for learners of all levels. Vocabulary words are grouped into beginner, intermediate and advanced, each with a clear meaning and an example sentence. The grammar questions explain why an answer is right, which is how mistakes turn into understanding. The general knowledge quiz covers science, geography, history and everyday facts, and the computer basics lessons cover safe habits that protect your accounts and files.

For your career, the Professional Growth Hub gathers practical advice on job hunting, professional communication, leadership and productivity. Interview Practice lets you rehearse real questions, write answer notes, time yourself and run a five-question mock round. You can also use the free resume builder and ATS resume checker to prepare your application.

Your scores, notes and saved thoughts are kept only in your own browser. Visit My Workspace to see them together, download a copy, or erase everything.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register?

No. Everything works without an account, and your progress is saved only in your browser.

Is it really free?

Yes. All activities are free to use.

Who writes the content?

The questions, tips and lessons are original material written for this website and checked for general accuracy. Always confirm important facts with an official source.
