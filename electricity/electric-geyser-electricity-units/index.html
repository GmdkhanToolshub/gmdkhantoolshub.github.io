Electricity units

How many units does a Electric Geyser use?

A Electric Geyser draws about 2,000 to 3,000 W for an electric water heater, so it uses about 3 units (kWh) for every hour it runs. At 1 hours a day that is about 3 units a day and 90 units a month.

Per hour3 units
Per day (1 h)3 units
Per month90 units
Typical power3,000 W

One unit on your bill is one kilowatt-hour (kWh): a 1,000 watt appliance running for one hour. The formula is units = watts × hours per day × days ÷ 1000. The wattage here is typical, so check the label or manual of your own appliance.

How to use fewer units

  • Use a timer or switch it off as soon as the water is hot.
  • Insulate the tank and pipes if you can.
  • A gas or solar water heater uses no electric units.

Frequently asked questions

How many units does a Electric Geyser use per hour?

At about 3,000 W, a Electric Geyser uses about 3 units (kWh) for every hour it runs. Check the label on your own appliance, because models differ.

How many units does a Electric Geyser use in a month?

Running 1 hours a day for 30 days, it uses about 90 units. Multiply by your price per unit for the cost.

How do I calculate the electricity cost of a Electric Geyser?

Units = watts × hours per day × days ÷ 1000. Cost = units × your price per unit. Use the calculator on this page or the full Electricity Bill Calculator.

Why does my bill not match this exactly?

Real bills include tariff slabs, fixed charges and taxes, and appliances do not always draw their rated power. Use the average rate printed on your own bill for the closest estimate.
