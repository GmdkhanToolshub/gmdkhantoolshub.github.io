How it works

  1. Tell me what you need. Use a Request a quote button, or email asmarkniazi@gmail.com with a short description, your deadline and any example files.
  2. Get a quote. I reply with the scope, price and timeline, in Pakistani rupees for clients in Pakistan or US dollars for international clients.
  3. Agree and start. Once you accept, we confirm the payment method and I begin the work.
  4. Review and receive. You review the work, I make the agreed changes, and you get the final files.

Why work with me

  • Real experience: I've worked in national sales operations, secondary sales administration, KPI reporting and project management at FMCG and consumer electronics companies. My full background is on my resume.
  • Accuracy first: I started my career in accounts and finance, so I check and reconcile numbers before they're reported.
  • Practical results: the dashboards, scorecards and reports I build are made to be used every week, not just presented once.
  • Clear communication: you get straightforward updates and a clear handover.

Prefer to do it yourself? The free tools on this site, including the Resume Builder, Excel Formula Generator and KPI Performance Calculator, are free to use.

What to include in your request

  • What you need, in a few sentences.
  • Your deadline.
  • For a resume: your current resume and one or two job adverts you're targeting.
  • For Excel or KPI work: a sample of your data or current file, with any confidential figures removed if you prefer.
  • Your budget range, if you have one. This is optional.

Frequently asked questions

How much do your services cost?

Every project is different, so I give a quote after understanding what you need. Clients in Pakistan are quoted in Pakistani rupees, and international clients in US dollars.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the job. The timeline is agreed with your quote before any work starts.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work with clients in Pakistan and abroad. We communicate by email and agree the payment method when you accept the quote.

What do you need from me to give a quote?

A short description of what you need, your deadline and, if possible, an example file or job advert. A budget range helps but is optional.

Are my files and information kept private?

Yes. Your files and information are used only for your project and are not shared with anyone else.
