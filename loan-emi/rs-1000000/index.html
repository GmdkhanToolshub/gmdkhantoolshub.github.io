Flat rate versus reducing balance, and balance over time

At 15% over 36 months a reducing-balance loan costs Rs 34,665 a month. A lender quoting a flat 15% would charge Rs 40,278 a month for the same loan, which is Rs 5,612 more each month, so always ask which method a quote uses.

Worked through for this loan: P = 1,000,000, r = 15% / 12 = 0.0125, n = 36, so EMI = 1,000,000 × 0.0125 × 1.012536 / (1.012536 − 1) = Rs 34,665. Your lender’s schedule may differ slightly, and fees and insurance are extra.

What is the monthly installment on a Rs 1,000,000 loan?

At 15% a year over 36 months the installment on Rs 1,000,000 is about Rs 34,665 a month (reducing balance). The table shows other rates and terms.

How much interest will I pay on Rs 1,000,000 over 3 years at 15%?

About Rs 247,952 in total interest on a reducing-balance loan, or Rs 450,000 if the lender charges a flat 15% rate.

Does a longer term lower the cost?

A longer term lowers the monthly installment but raises the total interest. On Rs 1,000,000 at 15%, 60 months costs Rs 427,396 in interest against Rs 247,952 over 36 months.
