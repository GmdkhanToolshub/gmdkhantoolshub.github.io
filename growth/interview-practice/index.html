How to use this

  1. Choose a question type or leave it on All, then read the question.
  2. Write key points in the notes box. Your notes are saved on this device only.
  3. Start the 2-minute timer and answer aloud. Then press Show how to answer for guidance.
  4. Try the Mock round for five random questions and rate yourself after each one.
  5. Open 30 sample interviews for model answers written for Pakistan, South and East Asia, the Gulf and worldwide, then press Practise this question.

About this page

The best way to prepare for an interview is to answer questions aloud. This page gives you realistic questions with short guidance on how to answer well, such as the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behavioural questions. Your notes are stored only in your browser and are never uploaded. Answers should always be truthful and based on your own experience. Guidance is general and each employer’s process is different.

Frequently asked questions

What is the STAR method?

A way to answer behavioural questions: describe the Situation, your Task, the Action you took and the Result.

Are my notes private?

Yes. They are saved only in your browser.

How to prepare answers that sound natural

Interviewers are not looking for perfect speeches; they want clear, honest examples. For questions about your experience, use the STAR method: describe the situation, the task you had, the action you took and the result. Keep each part to a sentence or two, and finish with a number or outcome if you can.

A short example

Question: tell me about a time you solved a problem. Answer: our weekly report kept arriving late because data came in three formats (situation). I was asked to fix it (task). I built a single Excel template and asked each team to use it (action). The report is now ready a day earlier (result).

Practice tips

  • Prepare three stories that you can adapt to different questions.
  • Practise out loud, and time yourself to stay under two minutes.
  • Research the company and prepare two questions to ask.
  • Read our guide to common interview questions in Pakistan and prepare your CV with the Resume Builder.
