FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All 58 tools and gadgets are free to use with no limits, no trial period and no sign-up. The site is supported by advertising and optional paid templates.

How is my data handled?

The tools process the numbers, text, resumes and images you enter in your browser, and they are not uploaded to our servers. Some tools save your work in your own browser so you can come back to it. The Currency Converter downloads exchange rates from an external service, and the site shows Google ads. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open any tool and start using it straight away.

Do the tools work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every tool is designed for phones first and also works on tablets and computers.

Can I type in Urdu?

Yes. Use the Urdu Keyboard, convert Roman Urdu to Urdu script, write amounts in words with Urdu digits, and convert dates to the Hijri calendar.

Can you build a custom tool, report or template for me?

Yes. I offer resume writing, Excel dashboards, KPI scorecards and sales operations consulting. See the Hire me page to request a quote.
