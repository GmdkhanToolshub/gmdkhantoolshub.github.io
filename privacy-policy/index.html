Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use this site and its free tools.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ghulam Mohiuddin Niazi (“I”, “me”, “this site”) handles information when you visit gmdkhantoolshub.github.io and use its free online tools. I aim to collect as little personal information as possible.

Information handled by the tools

The calculators, generators and converters run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. The text, numbers, resumes, images and other content you enter into a tool are not uploaded to or stored on my servers. Some tools save data in your own browser (for example your resume draft, favourite tools and recently used tools) using local storage so it is available when you return. You can delete this at any time from your browser settings or with the “Clear” buttons in the tools.

The Currency Converter requests exchange rates from a third-party service (Exchange Rate API). That request is made by your browser, so the provider can see your IP address and standard request data, but not the amounts you convert.

Contact form and email

If you use the contact form, the message you write is either opened in your own email app or, if a form service has been configured, sent through that service to my inbox. I use your name and email address only to reply to you, and I do not sell them or add them to mailing lists without your permission.

Analytics

This site is set up to use Google Analytics to understand which pages and tools are useful. Analytics cookies and scripts load only after you choose “Accept all” in the cookie banner. Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type. IP addresses are anonymised.

Advertising

This site shows advertising from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Advertising scripts load for all visitors, but ads are non-personalised unless you choose “Accept all”. Non-personalised ads can still use cookies for limits such as frequency capping and for fraud prevention. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are also asked for their choice through Google's consent message. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings and learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Cookies and local storage

Details of the cookies and storage used are in the Cookie Policy. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer.

Legal bases and your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the UK, EU/EEA or other regions with data-protection laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the tools do not send your content to me, there is usually little personal data to act on, but you can contact me at asmarkniazi@gmail.com with any request.

Third-party links

This site links to other websites such as LinkedIn. I am not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data retention and security

Messages sent to me are kept only as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping. The site is served over HTTPS where the hosting platform provides it, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy: asmarkniazi@gmail.com.
