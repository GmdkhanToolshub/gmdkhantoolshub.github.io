HR software for growing companies: employees, attendance, leave, payroll, recruitment and performance in one place. Explore the free demo today.

Everything HR in one place

GM HCM covers the seven areas most businesses manage in spreadsheets today.

Employee management

Employee profiles with ID, department, designation, reporting manager, joining date and employment history.

Attendance

Check-in and check-out, daily attendance, late arrivals, early departures, working hours and monthly reports.

Leave management

Annual, casual and sick leave, applications, approval or rejection, balances and history.

Payroll

Salary structures, allowances, deductions, overtime, monthly payroll and PDF payslips.

Recruitment

Job vacancies, applications, candidate tracking and interview scheduling.

Performance

KPIs, goals, reviews and manager feedback with performance reports.

Analytics and reports

Headcount, attendance, leave and payroll summaries with Excel and PDF export.

Why we are building GM HCM

Many small and medium companies keep employee data in scattered spreadsheets: one for attendance, another for leave, another for payroll. Mistakes creep in, files are shared by email, and it is hard to answer simple questions such as how many people are on leave this week or what the monthly payroll will be. GM HCM brings these tasks into one structured, role-based system that managers and employees can both use.

What the demo shows

  • Dashboard with headcount, attendance trend and department charts.
  • Employees list with search, filters and profiles.
  • Attendance grid with late and early-departure rules, and a check-in / check-out simulation.
  • Leave balances, applications and manager approval or rejection.
  • Payroll calculation with overtime and deductions, plus a downloadable sample payslip PDF.
  • Recruitment pipeline from applied to hired, with interview scheduling.
  • Performance goals and review ratings.

How the paid product is planned to protect your data

  • Each company has its own separate workspace.
  • Five roles: Super Administrator, Company Administrator, HR Administrator, Department Manager and Employee, each with limited permissions.
  • Subscriptions are activated only after payment has been verified on the server.
  • Audit logs record important administrative actions.

These are design goals for the paid platform, which is still being built. They are not features of the demo. We will publish the final privacy policy, subscription terms and security details before any company data is accepted.

Plans at a glance

Starter, Business and Enterprise plans are planned, with a free demo for everyone. See the pricing page for details, or request a quotation for a larger organisation.

Frequently asked questions

Is GM HCM available to buy today?

Not yet. The free demo is live now and shows how the product will work with sample data. Paid plans are being prepared. You can request early access and we will tell you by email what is available, the final pricing and the payment options.

Does the demo store my data?

No. The demo uses fictional sample data in your browser. Nothing you do is saved, sent or processed, and it resets when you refresh the page.

Are the prices final?

The plans and prices on this site are introductory and may change before launch. Annual billing is planned at ten months’ price for twelve months.

How will payments work?

The planned options are bank transfer with manual verification by our team, and card or local payment gateways once they are connected. A plan will only be activated after a payment has been verified on our servers.

How will company data be kept separate?

GM HCM is designed so that every company has its own workspace, with role-based access for company administrators, HR administrators, department managers and employees. The demo shows the screens; the secure multi-company platform is part of the paid product that is still being built.

Who is GM HCM for?

Small and growing businesses in Pakistan and elsewhere that want to move from spreadsheets to structured HR records, attendance, leave, payroll and recruitment in one place.
