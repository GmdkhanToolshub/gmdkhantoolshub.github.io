How we keep this list honest and current

  • Real sources only. Vacancies come from permitted job feeds (Arbeitnow, Remotive, Jobicy and Jooble today, and Careerjet for Pakistan and Adzuna once their free keys are connected). We do not scrape websites and we never create vacancies.
  • Strict dates. Every listing shows its publication date as given by the source. If an application deadline is published, the vacancy disappears when that day ends. If no deadline is published we say “Closing date not provided” and show the vacancy for at most 30 days after it was published. Older listings are never shown as new.
  • Checked links. Before a listing is published, its link is tested. Pages that return “not found” are removed, and duplicates are merged.
  • Updated automatically every six hours. The time of the last successful update is shown under the lists. If a source is unavailable, you see a message, never made-up jobs.
  • Salary and visa information are shown only when the source lists them. “Visa: not specified” means the listing does not say. Always confirm with the employer.

Pakistan vacancies

Pakistan has no free, open feed of job vacancies, and scraping job portals breaks their terms. The list above shows remote roles that are open to candidates in Pakistan, and the Careerjet Pakistan block lower on this page shows the latest vacancies in Pakistani cities, supplied live by Careerjet. You can also search directly on official and well-known Pakistani sources: Federal Public Service Commission, Rozee.pk and Mustakbil. GM is not affiliated with them.

Stay safe from job scams

  • Real employers never ask you to pay for an interview, a visa, a uniform or “registration”.
  • Be careful with offers that arrive on WhatsApp or from free email addresses, and with salaries that sound too good to be true.
  • Check the company on its own website and apply there. Never send passport, bank or card details before a written, verified offer.
  • For overseas jobs, check visa rules on the destination country's official government website.

Questions

Are these jobs real?

They come from established job feeds and each link is tested before it is shown. GM does not employ people and cannot guarantee any employer, so please verify before you apply.

Why do some jobs have no closing date?

Many employers do not publish one. We say so instead of inventing a date, and we hide those listings 30 days after they were published.

Does GM charge job seekers?

No. Browsing is free and no account is needed.

Which countries are covered?

Today the feeds cover Europe, the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia, the UAE and worldwide remote roles. Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Kuwait and Pakistani cities need the licensed feed described above.
