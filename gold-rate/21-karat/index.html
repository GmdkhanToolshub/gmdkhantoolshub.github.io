What decides the gold rate in Pakistan

Gold is priced worldwide in US dollars per troy ounce. Pakistani prices follow that international price, converted at the dollar rate, plus local premiums and taxes. When the dollar becomes more expensive in rupees, or when the international gold price rises, the price per tola rises too.

Karat explained

  • 24 karat: nearly pure gold (99.9%), used for bars and coins.
  • 22 karat: 91.6% gold, common for jewellery.
  • 21 karat: 87.5% gold, widely used for jewellery in Pakistan.
  • 18 karat: 75% gold, used for lighter and gem-set jewellery.

A tola is 11.6638 grams. Always ask for the weight and the karat in writing on your receipt.

Buying or selling gold safely

  • Compare at least two jewellers and ask for today's rate for the karat you want.
  • Ask for making charges and wastage separately, and check the weight on a scale in front of you.
  • Keep your receipt. When you sell, buyers usually deduct for wastage and stones, so the sale price is lower than the buying price.
  • Weigh and convert units with the Tola to Gram Converter.

Gold and zakat

Zakat on gold depends on the weight you hold. The usual gold nisab is 7.5 tola (87.48 grams). The Zakat Calculator helps you work it out, and a qualified scholar can answer questions about your own case.

About this page

Gold prices come from the free gold-api.com feed and dollar rates from Exchange Rate API. A scheduled job on this site collects them once a day. The figures are estimates for general information, not financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as the jeweller's gold rate in Pakistan?

No. This page shows an estimate from the international gold price converted to rupees. Local jewellers and bullion associations add their own premium and may differ by a few percent. Ask your jeweller for the exact rate.

How is this gold rate calculated?

The international price of one troy ounce of gold in US dollars is multiplied by the dollar to rupee rate, divided by 31.1035 grams in a troy ounce, and multiplied by 11.6638 grams in a tola. For lower karats the result is scaled by karat divided by 24.

What is 21 karat gold?

21 karat gold is 21 parts pure gold out of 24, which is 87.5% gold.

How often is the rate updated?

Once a day by an automated job. For a live price, ask your jeweller or check a bullion association.

Does the price include making charges?

No. Making charges, wastage, stones and taxes are extra and differ between shops.

How many grams are in one tola?

One tola is 11.6638 grams. It has 12 masha, and each masha has 8 ratti.
