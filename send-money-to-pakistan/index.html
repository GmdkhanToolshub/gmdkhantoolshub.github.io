Send Money to Pakistan: Compare the Real Cost

See how many rupees your family actually receives after the transfer fee and the exchange-rate margin. Works for USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, CAD, AUD and QAR.

Hidden cost = how much less the receiver gets compared with converting the same amount at the market rate with no fee. Your numbers stay in your browser.

The three numbers that decide what your family receives

  1. The fee the provider charges, often shown clearly.
  2. The exchange rate the provider uses. A lower rate than the market rate is a hidden fee.
  3. Speed and payout method: bank deposit, mobile wallet or cash pickup. A cheaper option that arrives late or is hard to collect may cost your family more in practice.

A short checklist before you send

  • Compare at least three options at the same time of day, using the same amount.
  • Ask for the final rupee amount the receiver will get, in writing or on screen.
  • Check that the provider is licensed or registered where you live and works with a reputable payout partner in Pakistan.
  • Double-check the receiver's name, account number or wallet before you press send.
  • Keep the receipt and tracking reference until the money is collected.

Why the official rate and the rate you are offered differ

Banks and money-transfer companies buy foreign currency at one price and sell it at a slightly different price, then add their own margin. That is why the rate on a transfer screen is almost always lower than the rate you see in a news headline. Tracking the market rate helps you notice a margin that is unusually large. Frequently asked questions

How do I find the cheapest way to send money to Pakistan?

Compare the total rupees the receiver gets, not just the fee. A "zero fee" transfer can still cost more if the exchange rate is lower than the market rate. Enter each provider's fee and the exchange rate it shows into the calculator above and compare the "rupees received" and "hidden cost" numbers.

What is the mid-market exchange rate?

It is the rate banks use between themselves, halfway between the buy and sell price. Consumers rarely get exactly this rate. The difference between the mid-market rate and the rate you are offered is the exchange-rate margin, and it is often the biggest part of the cost.

Where does the calculator get its rates?

From the free open.er-api.com exchange-rate feed, collected once a day by an automated job on this site. They are estimates for comparison, not a guaranteed rate. Always use the rate the provider shows you before you confirm a transfer.

Does the amount arrive instantly?

It depends on the provider, the payout method (bank account, mobile wallet or cash pickup), the amount and checks the provider must carry out. Ask the provider for the expected delivery time and check it again before you send.

How can I avoid scams when sending money abroad?

Use providers that are licensed or registered in the country you send from. Check the receiver's name and account details carefully, never send money to a stranger or for a prize or job fee, and keep your receipt and reference number. See our guide on staying safe from scams.
