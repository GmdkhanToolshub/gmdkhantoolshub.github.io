Which rate do you need?

  • Dollar (USD): freelance income, imports, savings and many remittances.
  • Dirham, riyal (AED, SAR, QAR): Gulf salaries and money sent home. These currencies are pegged to the dollar, so their rates move almost in step with the dollar rate.
  • Pound, euro (GBP, EUR): study, work and family in the UK and Europe.
  • Canadian and Australian dollar (CAD, AUD): students, migration and families in Canada and Australia.

How to read an exchange rate

A rate of 277 means one unit of the foreign currency buys 277 Pakistani rupees. A higher number means the rupee is weaker against that currency. The rate you actually get depends on the bank or service, so always compare the final rupee amount.

Frequently asked questions

Where do these rates come from?

They come from the open data feed of Exchange Rate API and are collected once a day by an automated job on this site. They are mid-market reference rates, not the State Bank of Pakistan rate.

Why do banks and exchange companies show different rates?

They add a margin to the mid-market rate and may charge a fee. Always compare the final rupee amount before you exchange or send money.
