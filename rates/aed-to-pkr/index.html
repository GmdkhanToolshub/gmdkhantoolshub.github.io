What the AED to PKR rate means

The AED to PKR rate tells you how many Pakistani rupees one UAE dirham buys. When the number goes up, the rupee has become weaker against the dirham. When it goes down, the rupee has become stronger.

The dirham matters to the many Pakistanis working in the United Arab Emirates and sending money home. The dirham is pegged to the US dollar at about 3.6725 dirhams per dollar, so the AED to PKR rate moves almost in step with the USD to PKR rate.

Why the rate you get is different

The rate on this page is the mid-market rate, the middle point between buying and selling prices that banks use between themselves. It is a fair reference, but a bank, exchange company or transfer app gives a slightly different rate and keeps the gap, and some also charge a fee. In Pakistan the open-market rate can differ a little from the interbank rate. Always ask for the exact amount in rupees before you send or exchange money.

Tips when you exchange or send money

  • Compare the final amount in rupees, not just the fee. A "no fee" offer can hide a poor rate.
  • Use regulated banks, exchange companies and licensed apps, and keep your receipts.
  • Compare services with the Send Money to Pakistan Calculator.
  • For any other currency use the Currency Converter.
  • Gulf End of Service Gratuity Calculator

About these rates

Rates come from the open data feed of Exchange Rate API and are collected once a day by an automated job on this site. They are for general information, are not the official State Bank of Pakistan rate, and are not financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

What is the AED to PKR rate today?

The rate for today is shown at the top of this page and is refreshed once a day. It is the mid-market rate, a fair reference between the buying and selling prices.

Is this the same as the bank or open-market rate?

Not exactly. A bank, exchange company or money transfer app gives a slightly different rate and keeps the difference, and the open-market rate in Pakistan can also differ a little. Ask for the exact rupee amount before you send or exchange money.

Why does the AED rate follow the dollar rate?

The dirham is pegged to the US dollar at about 3.6725 dirhams per dollar, so the AED to PKR rate moves almost in step with the USD to PKR rate.

Why is the rate here different from other websites?

Websites use different data sources and update at different times. The rates on this page come from one public data feed collected once a day.

How can I get more rupees when I send UAE dirham home?

Compare the final amount in rupees from each service after fees and exchange rate margin. The Send Money to Pakistan Calculator on this site compares up to three services you enter.

Is this the State Bank of Pakistan rate?

No. These are market reference rates for general information, not the official State Bank of Pakistan rate, and they are not financial advice.
