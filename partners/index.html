Partner with GM

Work with an independent, transparent digital business that builds Excel dashboards, HR trackers and free tools for people around the world.

Ways to partner with GM

We work with accountants, consultants, agencies, software companies, training institutes and creators who serve small businesses and job seekers around the world.

Referral partners

Introduce clients who need dashboards, reporting or HR tracking. Any referral terms are agreed in writing first.

Template resellers and co-branded templates

Offer GM Excel templates to your customers, or have a template adapted with your branding.

Sponsored content

Clearly labelled sponsored articles and tool sponsorships. See Advertise with us.

Content and training

Joint guides, webinars and workshops on Excel reporting, HR records and job-search skills.

Software and tool partners

Honest reviews and recommendations of products that help GM readers. Affiliate links are always disclosed.

Something else

Have another idea that helps both audiences? Tell us what you have in mind.

What GM brings

  • Original products. Tested Excel templates, free Lite versions and a library of 58 free tools.
  • Real experience. More than nine years in sales operations and reporting.
  • Remote and clear. Written scope and fixed prices in US dollars.
  • Honest marketing. No fake reviews, no purchased traffic and no guaranteed-income claims.

How we work together

  1. Introduce yourself. Tell us who you serve and what you want to achieve.
  2. Fit check. We check that the partnership helps both audiences.
  3. Written agreement. Scope, responsibilities, any fees or commission, and how to end it.
  4. Launch and review. We agree how results are measured and review them together.

No commitments, payments or public statements are made until both sides agree in writing. GM does not send unsolicited bulk messages.
