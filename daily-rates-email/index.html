Daily Gold, Silver & Dollar Rates by Email

One short email with Pakistan's estimated gold (per tola), silver and USD to PKR rates and what changed since yesterday. Free, and you can unsubscribe any time.

Please read: the rates are estimates from international prices and the exchange rate, not a jeweller's, bullion association's or bank's quote.

Join the daily rates email

By subscribing you agree to receive these emails. You will get a confirmation email first. Read the Privacy Policy.

What you get

  • Gold 24 karat and 22 karat, per tola, with the change since the previous day.
  • Silver per tola.
  • US dollar to rupee, plus links for GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, CAD, AUD and QAR.
  • A link to the full gold rate, silver rate and dollar rate pages.

Who it is for

Families who buy gold for weddings or savings, jewellers, traders, overseas Pakistanis and anyone who wants the numbers without opening a website every day. If you send money home, try the send money to Pakistan calculator too.

For businesses

A small "presented by" line for a jeweller, exchange dealer or finance business can be arranged when the list is large enough to be useful. There are no subscriber numbers to quote yet and none will be invented. See Advertise with us.

Frequently asked questions

What is in the daily rates email?

A short summary of the latest estimated 24K and 22K gold price per tola, silver per tola and the US dollar to rupee rate, with the change since the previous day and a link to the full tables and calculators.

Are these official rates?

No. They are estimates worked out from international gold and silver prices and the exchange rate. Local jewellers and bullion associations add their own premium and may differ by a few percent. Always confirm the exact rate with your jeweller before you buy or sell.

How often will I get an email?

The plan is one short email on days when the rates are updated. The first emails may be sent a few days after launch while the format is tested. You can unsubscribe with one click from any email.

What do you do with my email address?

It is used only to send these updates through the Buttondown email service. We do not sell or rent your address. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Can a business sponsor the email?

Yes, a small "presented by" line can be arranged once the list is large enough to be useful. We will share real subscriber numbers when there is something honest to report. Contact us through the Advertise page.
