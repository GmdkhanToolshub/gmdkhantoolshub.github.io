Inventory & Sales Tracker

Track stock in and out, sales and profit for a small shop or business.

Features

  • Current stock, reorder and out-of-stock status for each product
  • Profit on each sale, with an optional price and discount per sale
  • Stock value at cost, top 5 products by revenue
  • Monthly sales and profit with a chart

Limits: please read before ordering

  • Up to 200 products
  • Up to 1,000 rows of stock received and 1,000 rows of sales
  • One cost price per product: no batch, weighted-average or first-in-first-out costing
  • No batch, expiry or multi-warehouse tracking

How to use it

  1. Enter your business name, currency label and year on Settings.
  2. List each product once with a unique code.
  3. Record deliveries on Stock In and every sale on Sales.
  4. Delete the rows marked Example first.

What you need

Microsoft Excel on a computer. The workbook has no macros. On 25 November 2026 it was opened and recalculated in Microsoft Excel (version 16.0) on Windows, and a test entry in the last supported row was picked up correctly. Not tested: older Excel versions, Excel for Mac, web or mobile, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.
