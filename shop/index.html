How ordering works

Simple, manual and safe. Nobody can pay the wrong account, because payment details are sent to you privately.

  1. Choose a product and press Order on WhatsApp or Order by email. A ready-made message opens for you to send.
  2. GM confirms your order. After confirming availability, GM sends you the payment details privately, by WhatsApp or email.
  3. Pay by JazzCash or bank transfer. Bank transfers from any bank in Pakistan are accepted, and foreign transfers can be discussed in USD or other currencies.
  4. Send proof of payment (a screenshot) by WhatsApp or email.
  5. Receive your file. It is sent to you after the payment is confirmed.

Stay safe from scammers. GM does not publish account numbers on this website. Pay only to details that GM sends you directly in reply to your own order, and check them against the confirmation. GM will never ask for your card number, PIN, OTP or online-banking password.
