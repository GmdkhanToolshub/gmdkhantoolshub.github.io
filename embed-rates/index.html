Live Gold, Silver & Dollar Rates for Your Website

Add Pakistan's estimated gold (24K and 22K per tola), silver and USD to PKR rates to any website with one line of code. Free, no sign-up, updated every day, in English or Urdu.

Please read: the numbers are estimates from international gold and silver prices and the dollar to rupee rate. They are not a jeweller's, bullion association's or bank's quote, and they update once a day, not live. The widget states this on screen.

What the widget shows

  • Gold, 24 karat and 22 karat, per tola (one tola is 11.6638 grams).
  • Silver, per tola.
  • US dollar to Pakistani rupee.
  • The change since the previous day, the date of the last update and a plain "estimate" notice.

Where the numbers come from

Gold and silver prices in US dollars per troy ounce come from the free gold-api.com feed and the dollar rate from Exchange Rate API. A scheduled job on this site collects them once a day and converts them to rupees per tola. The same figures power the gold rate, silver rate and USD to PKR pages, which also show the daily history and how each figure is calculated.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Pakistan rates widget really free?

Yes. You can add it to a website or blog for free, with no account and no sign-up. The widget shows a small "Rates by GM Tools Hub" link, which you should keep in place.

Are these official gold and dollar rates?

No. They are estimates worked out from the international gold and silver price and the dollar to rupee exchange rate. Local jewellers, bullion associations and banks add their own premiums and may differ by a few percent. The widget says this on every display.

How often do the numbers update?

Once a day, by an automated job that collects the international prices and the exchange rate. The widget shows the date of the last update and the change since the previous day. It is not a live tick-by-tick price.

How is the gold rate per tola calculated?

The international price of one troy ounce of gold in US dollars is multiplied by the dollar to rupee rate, divided by 31.1035 grams per troy ounce, and multiplied by 11.6638 grams per tola. For 22 karat the result is multiplied by 22 and divided by 24.

Can I show the rates in Urdu or in dark mode?

Yes. Choose Urdu in the builder on this page, or add lang=ur to the widget address. Add theme=dark for dark mode and accent with a six-digit colour code to match your site.

Can I remove the credit link or add my shop name?

The free widget keeps the credit link. If you want a branded version, for example "Rates presented by your shop" with your colours and no credit, contact me and we can arrange a custom version. Prices are agreed in writing before any work starts.

Does the widget slow down my site or track my visitors?

The widget is a small page loaded in an iframe, with no advertising or tracking scripts of its own and no cookies set by the widget. It reads three small data files from gmtoolshub.com once when it loads.
