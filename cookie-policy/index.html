Cookie Policy

The cookies and browser storage used on this site and how to control them.

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on gmdkhantoolshub.github.io.

What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that keeps data on your device. Both help a site remember choices and measure how it is used.

Essential storage (always on)

These items make the site work the way you expect. They stay on your device and are not used for tracking.

NamePurposeDuration
niazi.themeRemembers light or dark modeUntil cleared
niazi.consentRemembers your cookie choiceUntil cleared

Analytics (only if you accept all)

If Google Analytics is enabled and you choose “Accept all”, Google sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visitors and measure usage. Typical lifetime is up to two years. See Google's cookie information.

Advertising

This site shows Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may set cookies (for example __gads and __gpi) to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. If you choose “Accept all”, ads may also be personalised; otherwise they are not. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for their choice through Google's consent message. Manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Managing your choice

You can change your decision at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer, or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not stop the tools working.
