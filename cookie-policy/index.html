Cookie Policy

The cookies and browser storage used on this site and how to control them.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar technologies used on gmdkhantoolshub.github.io.

What are cookies and local storage?

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature that keeps data on your device. Both help a site remember choices and measure how it is used.

These items make the site work the way you expect. They stay on your device and are not used for tracking.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-TNH5CHZ8YB) to count visits and see which pages and tools are useful. Google sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to distinguish visitors; the typical lifetime is up to two years. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: analytics cookies are not set unless you agree, and Google may receive limited, cookieless signals. Everywhere else, analytics runs unless you choose “Non-personalised only” from the “Cookie settings” link in the footer, which turns it off. Analytics never runs in Kids Corner. IP addresses are anonymised and Google signals and ad personalisation are switched off for analytics. See Google's cookie information.

Advertising

This site shows Google AdSense ads. Google and its partners may set cookies (for example __gads and __gpi) to show and measure ads, limit how often you see them and prevent fraud. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for their choice through Google's certified consent message, and ads are only personalised if they agree. Elsewhere, ads may be personalised unless you choose “Non-personalised only” from the “Cookie settings” link. Manage personalised ads at Google Ads Settings.

Third-party services

Third-party services this site contacts: Google's advertising scripts (Google AdSense, including Google's consent message for visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), described in the Advertising section; and ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com), which the Currency Converter contacts to download the latest exchange-rate table. Only the request for rates is sent, never the amounts you enter. Share buttons, translation links and Gumroad links contact those services only if you click them. Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) provides the weather on the Free Tools page. Your browser contacts it for the forecast, and, if you press “Use my location”, it also receives your approximate coordinates. Careerjet (careerjet.org) provides the Pakistan jobs widget on the Jobs page and is loaded only when you press “Show Careerjet jobs” or if you have already accepted cookies. Careerjet may set its own cookies under its own policy. Fonts and all other scripts are served from this site. ExchangeRate-API and Open-Meteo do not set cookies through this site.

Managing your choice

You can change your decision at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer, or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking cookies does not stop the tools working.
