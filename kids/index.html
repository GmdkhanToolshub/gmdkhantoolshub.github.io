What can children make?

The Drawing Studio is a simple digital canvas with big, friendly buttons. Children can sketch with a pencil, paint with a soft brush, add rainbow lines, fill shapes with colour using the paint bucket, stamp animals, faces and space stickers, and add their own text to a picture. Backgrounds such as sky, meadow, ocean and space turn a plain page into a scene, which makes it easy to create a cartoon story from a few stickers and some drawing.

Every tool has a large picture button and a short name, so younger children can recognise it. Mistakes are never a problem: Undo and Redo are always at hand, and Clear removes the drawing with one tap. When a picture is finished, it can be saved to the private gallery, downloaded as a high-quality JPEG, PNG or PDF, or printed.

The colouring pages are built for the paint bucket: each page is made of closed shapes, so one tap fills a whole area. Older children can add their own details with the brushes on top. Drawing challenges give a starting idea for ages 4–6, 7–9 and 10 and over, which is helpful when a child says “I don’t know what to draw”.

For parents and carers

Safe, private and ad-free by design

Kids Corner is built so children can be creative without being tracked, contacted or shown adverts.

  • No accountsChildren never sign up, log in or enter a name, email address or age.
  • No chat or public sharingThere are no comments, public profiles, messages or ways for strangers to reach a child.
  • Private by defaultPictures are saved only inside this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.
  • No adverts in Kids CornerWe do not show advertising or load ad scripts on any Kids Corner page.
  • No location or trackingThe drawing tools do not use location, camera or microphone.
  • You stay in controlYou can delete any picture, or every saved picture, from the gallery at any time.

Tips for a good creative session

  • Sit with younger children the first time and let them explore the tools.
  • Use the drawing challenges for a short, fun activity of 10 minutes.
  • Print finished work or save it as a PDF to share with family offline.
  • Take regular screen breaks and encourage drawing on paper too.
  • Other pages on this website, such as the tools, are made for adults. Kids Corner links are kept separate for children.

Because pictures live in this browser, clearing browser data or using a different device will remove them. Download a JPEG or PDF to keep a permanent copy.

Frequently asked questions

Do children need an account?

No. There are no accounts, logins or forms. Children can start drawing immediately.

Where are the pictures stored?

Only in your own browser, on your own device. We do not receive or store them.

Can pictures be printed or saved?

Yes. Each picture can be downloaded as a JPEG, PNG or PDF, or printed straight from the studio.

Are there adverts?

No. Kids Corner pages do not show adverts or load advertising scripts.

Which devices work best?

Any modern phone, tablet or computer. A finger, stylus or mouse all work for drawing.
