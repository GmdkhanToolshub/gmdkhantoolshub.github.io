Brain Booster

Train memory, patterns and logic with short, fun challenges. Choose beginner, intermediate or advanced.

Brain Booster

How to use this

  1. Pick a game: patterns, odd one out, copy the colours or riddles.
  2. Choose a level, then tap your answer.
  3. In Copy the colours, watch the lights and repeat them in the same order.
  4. Every correct answer earns stars. Take a break when you feel tired.

About this page

Brain Booster has four short games. In patterns, children find what comes next in a row of pictures or numbers. In odd one out, they spot the item that does not belong, which trains grouping and reasoning. Copy the colours is a memory game that grows by one colour each round, and riddles encourage careful thinking and language. Beginner questions are gentle, and advanced questions use longer sequences, number patterns and trickier groups. Nothing is timed, so children can think at their own pace.

Frequently asked questions

Do children need an account?

No. Nothing needs a login. Stars and badges are saved only on this device.

Is anything collected about my child?

No. Kids Corner does not ask for names, ages, photos or contact details, and it has no chat, no adverts and no analytics.

Are the games timed?

No. There is no countdown, so children can take their time.

How does the colour game work?

It plays a short sequence of lit colours. Repeat it in the same order. Each round adds one more colour.
