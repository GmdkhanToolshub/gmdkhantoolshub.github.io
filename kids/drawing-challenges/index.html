How to use this

  1. Choose the age group, then press New challenge for an idea.
  2. Draw it in the studio below with brushes, shapes, stickers and text.
  3. Optional: start the 10-minute timer for a quick-draw challenge.
  4. Save your picture to your gallery or download it when you are done.

About this page

Challenges are short prompts such as “Draw a smiling sun” or “Draw a three-picture comic strip”. There are no scores and no right or wrong answers, because the goal is to enjoy being creative. Parents can use the timer for a calm 10-minute activity, and older children can try the same prompt again using only three colours.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a prize or score?

No. The challenges are for fun and practice only.

Can I draw something different?

Of course. The idea is just a starting point.

Are there adverts?

No. Kids Corner pages do not show adverts or load advertising scripts.
