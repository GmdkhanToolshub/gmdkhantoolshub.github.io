Colouring Pages

Choose a picture, pick a colour and tap inside each shape to fill it. Save, download as PDF or print.

How to use this

  1. Choose a picture from “Pictures to colour” on the right: animals, nature, space or cartoons.
  2. The paint bucket is selected for you. Pick a colour from the palette.
  3. Tap inside a shape to fill it. Use Undo if you change your mind, or draw extra details with the brushes.
  4. Save to your gallery, download a JPEG or PDF, or print the finished page.

About this page

Each colouring page is drawn from closed shapes with thick lines, so the paint bucket fills exactly one area at a time. If a tap lands on a black line nothing happens, so tap inside the shape instead. You can paint over the lines with the brushes, but the black outline always stays on top of your colours, so the picture stays neat. To print a colouring page for someone who prefers paper, press Print before colouring.

Frequently asked questions

Which pictures are included?

Cat, fish, butterfly, flower garden, tree and sun, rocket, planet and stars, robot, car and house.

Why does the paint bucket not fill?

Tap inside a shape, not on a black line. Very small gaps between lines can also stop colour from spreading, so try another spot.

Are there adverts?

No. Kids Corner pages do not show adverts or load advertising scripts.
