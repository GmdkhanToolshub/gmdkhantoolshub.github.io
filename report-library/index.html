Questions

Are the example workbooks free to use?

Yes. You can download and change them for your own work. The data in them is made up, so replace it and check your results before sharing a report.

What is the difference between an example and a paid template?

An example teaches one method and is linked to a guide. A paid template is built for repeated use, with more rows and sheets. Each product page lists its features and limits.

Have the files been tested?

The free workbooks listed here were opened and recalculated in Microsoft Excel (version 16.0) without error cells, external links or macros. Other spreadsheet programs were not tested.

Do I need an account?

No. Free tools and downloads need no sign-up.
