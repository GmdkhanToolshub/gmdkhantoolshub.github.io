Free Reasoning Test: IQ-Style Pattern Puzzles

Twenty pattern puzzles from easy to hard. Free, no sign-up, and your answers stay in your browser.

How the test works

Every puzzle shows a 3 by 3 grid with the last piece missing. Look along each row to see what changes: the shape, how many there are, whether it is outlined, striped or solid, how big it is, and whether it has a dot or bar. Then choose the option that completes the grid. The first puzzles change one thing; the last ones change four at once.

What the result means, honestly

This is a reasoning quiz, not a clinical IQ test. IQ tests are standardised on large, representative groups before they can say how you compare with others, and this one has not been. So you get a score out of 100 (harder puzzles count more) and a level for this test only. There is no IQ number and no percentile. If you want a clinical assessment, ask a qualified psychologist.

Tips

  • Check one attribute at a time across a row: first the shape, then the count, and so on.
  • Some rules repeat in a row, some step up, and some use each value once per row.
  • Cross out options that break a rule you are sure of.

Certified test and certificate

The certified test has 30 puzzles and a 35-minute limit and is scored on our server. Afterwards you can buy a shareable Certificate of Completion with a verification ID that anyone can check at the verification page. The certificate states that it is not a clinical IQ assessment. Paid certificates open when online checkout is switched on.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a real IQ test?

No. It is a pattern-reasoning quiz in the style of matrix puzzles used in many cognitive tests, but it has not been standardised on a representative group of people, so it cannot give a valid IQ score. We show a score out of 100 and a level for this test only, and we never show an IQ number.

What do the puzzles measure?

Each puzzle is a 3 by 3 grid with one piece missing. You work out the rules that change along the rows (shape, number, fill, size, marker) and pick the piece that completes the grid. This exercises pattern spotting and logical reasoning with unfamiliar problems.

How is the score worked out?

Harder puzzles count more: easy puzzles are worth 1 point, then 2, 3 and 4 for the hardest. Your score is the points you earned divided by the total, as a percentage. The level (Developing, Average, Above average, High, Very high) is a band of that score on this test only.

Why is there no percentile or ranking?

A fair percentile needs results from a large number of people. We do not have that yet, so we do not show one. If we publish percentiles later, they will be based on real results and say how many people they come from.

What is the certified test and certificate?

The certified test has 30 puzzles, a 35-minute limit and is scored on our server so the result can be trusted. After you finish you can buy a Certificate of Completion with your name, score, level, date and a verification ID anyone can check. It says clearly that it is not a clinical IQ assessment. Paid certificates are not available until online checkout is switched on.

Do you keep my answers?

The practice test runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere. The certified test sends your answers to our server to score them and keeps the result, so a certificate can be verified. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I retake it?

Yes. The practice test is free and unlimited. A certificate is tied to one certified attempt.
