Subscription Policy

How paid GM HCM plans work: billing, payment verification, renewal, expiry and cancellation. Effective when paid plans open.

Last updated 8 November 2026. This policy applies to paid GM HCM plans, which are still being prepared. The free tools, Kids Corner and the GM HCM demo are free and need no subscription.

Plans

GM HCM is offered as a Free Demo, Starter, Business and Enterprise plan. Each plan lists its features and employee capacity on the pricing page. Prices are shown in Pakistani rupees, are introductory and may change before launch. A price change will not affect a subscription period you have already paid for.

Billing periods

Plans can be purchased monthly or annually. Annual billing is planned at ten months’ price for twelve months. A subscription period starts on the day your plan is activated.

Payment and activation

Planned payment methods are bank transfer with manual verification by our team, and card or local payment gateways once they are connected. A plan is activated only after we have verified the payment on our servers or in our bank account. Selecting a plan or pressing a button on the website does not activate it. We will send a confirmation and a receipt or invoice once payment is verified.

Renewal and expiry

Subscriptions do not renew without a payment. Before a plan expires we will remind you by email. If a plan expires, the workspace becomes read-only for a grace period, then access is suspended until renewal. Your data is not deleted immediately; see data export below.

Failed or rejected payments

If a payment cannot be verified, the plan is not activated and we will contact you using the email address you provided. You can retry with another payment method.

Cancellation

You can cancel at any time by contacting us. Cancellation stops future renewals and your plan continues until the end of the period you have paid for. Refunds are described in the Refund Policy.

Data export and deletion

You can request an export of your company data, in common formats such as Excel and PDF, while your subscription is active and for a period after it ends that we will state before launch. After that period you can ask us to delete your data, and we will do so unless the law requires us to keep some records, such as invoices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as GM HCM launches. Material changes will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about subscriptions: use the contact page.
