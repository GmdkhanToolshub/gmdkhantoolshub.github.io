Questions

Can I buy Pro or Agency today?

No. Both are planned and not on sale. No payment is taken and nothing renews. You can join the waiting list, which only opens a message in your own WhatsApp or email.

Are the free tools really free?

Yes. The online tools are free to use without registration. Free embeds on your own website keep a GM Tools Hub credit link.

How are digital products priced?

Templates and packs are one-time purchases with prices in US dollars and rupees shown on each product. They are not part of any monthly plan.

How do I order a product?

Use the order button to message on WhatsApp or email. Payment details are confirmed there and the file is delivered after payment is confirmed. See the Refund Policy.

What about the existing memberships?

Template Club and Career Pro are separate optional offers, delivered by hand with no automatic renewal. Their current prices are on the Membership page.
