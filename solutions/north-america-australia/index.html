North America and Australia

Dashboards and trackers for North American and Australian teams

A reliable remote reporting partner: clear written briefs, fixed USD quotes and overnight progress.

Start-ups, family businesses and small distributors in the United States, Canada and Australia often need a specialist for a few weeks rather than a full-time hire. GM works remotely and in writing, so a brief you send at the end of your day can be waiting for you, worked on, the next morning.

The time difference is large, so the workflow is built for it: a written brief, a short call to confirm scope, then clear written updates and a review call at a time that suits you. There is no need for you to be online while the work is done.

What businesses in this region ask for

Founder dashboards for start-ups

A single view of sales, costs and targets that a founder can update in minutes each week.

Distributor and route sales reports

Territory, rep and product reporting with targets and run-rate for small wholesalers and distributors.

HR attendance for 10 to 50 staff

Exception-based absence and leave tracking with balances and monthly attendance rates.

Inventory tracking for small retailers

Stock levels, reorder alerts and profit per product without a monthly platform fee.

Consulting and services reports

Project and client reporting for small professional-services firms.

Template customisation

Pick a GM template and have it adapted to your business at a fixed price.

Why companies choose to work with GM

  • Asynchronous by design. Written briefs, written updates, and review calls at your convenience.
  • Fixed price in US dollars. You approve the scope and price before work begins.
  • No subscriptions. You receive an Excel file you own.
  • Plain communication. Short, clear updates, without jargon.
  • Real operations experience. More than nine years in sales operations, distribution and performance reporting.

Questions

How do you handle the time difference?

Most work is asynchronous. We agree the brief, then you receive written updates and a review call is booked at a time that suits you.

How do I pay?

Payment terms are written in your quote. International clients normally pay in US dollars by bank transfer. We can discuss other options when you request a quote.

Do you provide Google Sheets versions?

Deliverables are Microsoft Excel workbooks. Google Sheets versions can be discussed, but some features may differ, so please ask first.

Can I see something before I commit?

Yes. Try the free Lite templates, or ask for a short description or screenshots of a similar finished project.

Request a quote

One short form. You get a written fixed-price quote, and you decide whether to go ahead.
