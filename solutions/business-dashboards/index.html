Business dashboards

Excel dashboards that answer real questions

A good dashboard shows what needs attention today. We start from the questions you ask in meetings, define each measure, and only then draw charts.

When this helps

  • Managers ask the same questions and wait for someone to prepare the answer.
  • Two people report different numbers for the same measure.
  • The current dashboard is full of charts but shows no action to take.
  • Adding a month means rebuilding the layout.

What you get

  • A one-page layout that puts the most important measures first
  • A written definition for every measure: formula, source column and exclusions
  • Filters for the views you need, such as region, product or month
  • Source data kept on separate sheets, with a simple way to add new rows

Example: questions turned into measures

Question askedMeasureDefinition to agree first
Are we on target?Target achievement %Actual sales ÷ target for the same days and products
How much more do we need per day?Required daily run rate(Target − actual) ÷ working days left
Which outlets stopped buying?Lost active outletsBought last month, nothing this month
Is stock reaching the market?Secondary to primary ratioDistributor sales to outlets ÷ sales to the distributor

What to send us

  • A sample of your data table with column headings
  • Three to five questions the dashboard must answer
  • Who will read it and on what screen

Remove names, phone numbers, ID numbers and bank details before sharing any file. Never send a password.

What is not included

These are Excel dashboards. Power BI or web dashboards are quoted separately, only after we confirm they can be delivered to your standard.

Which option fits

One clean table and up to five measures fits the $99 package. Several sources or report pages fit the $199 package. See the sample dashboard to judge the style first.

Questions

Can I see an example before paying?

Yes. The FMCG sample dashboard on this site is built from made-up data and can be downloaded, so you can check the formulas yourself.

How many charts will I get?

As few as answer your questions. The package limits measures and pages rather than charts, because extra charts often hide the important one.

Who keeps the dashboard up to date?

You do, using the handover note. Ongoing monthly updating is a separate arrangement agreed in writing.

Do you need access to our systems?

No. A sample or anonymised export is enough. Do not share passwords.
