What we build

Practical systems for the way your business already works

From a single dashboard to a complete monthly reporting pack. Built in Excel, so your team can use it on day one.

Sales and distribution dashboards

See sales against target by territory, salesperson and product, with run-rate and month-end projections. Covers primary and secondary sales.

Typical deliverable: Excel dashboard with refresh guide.

KPI scorecards and performance reporting

Weighted scorecards with fair ratings and rankings, so reviews are based on the same numbers every month.

Typical deliverable: scorecard workbook and review template.

HR attendance and leave systems

Log exceptions only, track leave balances and see attendance rates. Supports Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday, or Sunday-only weekends.

Typical deliverable: tracker for 10 to 200 staff.

Inventory and stock tracking

Stock in and out, reorder alerts, profit per sale and your best-selling products, without buying expensive software.

Typical deliverable: inventory and sales workbook.

Management reporting packs

Turn raw exports from your system into one clean monthly summary that owners and managers actually read.

Typical deliverable: monthly pack template with notes.

Excel automation and clean-up

Remove copy-and-paste work, merge many files into one, add data checks and document how everything works.

Typical deliverable: tidy, documented workbook.

How a project works

  1. Tell us what you need. Use the form below. A short description is enough.
  2. Get a written quote. Scope, timeline and price in US dollars, with nothing hidden.
  3. Approve and share your data. Work starts only after you accept the quote. Sample or anonymised data is fine.
  4. Review and refine. You see progress, give feedback and approve the result.
  5. Handover. You receive the finished file, a short guide and a walkthrough. Payment terms (for example part payment before work and the balance on delivery) are written in your quote.

Questions

Do you work with companies outside Pakistan?

Yes. Projects are delivered remotely by email and online meetings, and invoiced in US dollars. See the pages for the Gulf, UK and Europe and North America and Australia.

What will it cost?

Every project is different, so you receive a written fixed-price quote after a short discussion. Customising a ready-made template is usually quicker and cheaper than a build from scratch.

Is my business data safe?

Your files are used only for your project and are never shared. You can send anonymised or sample data first. A confidentiality agreement can be signed on request.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of what you track today, what you want to see, a sample (or anonymised) export from your current system, and your deadline.

Do I need special software?

Deliverables are Microsoft Excel workbooks (Excel 2010 or later) that work offline. There is no subscription and no lock-in: you own the file.

Can you guarantee results such as more sales?

No. We build clear, accurate reporting and tools so you can see what is happening and decide faster. We do not promise business results.
