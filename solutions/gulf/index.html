Gulf region

Reporting and HR systems for Gulf businesses

Sales dashboards, stock tracking and attendance records that follow your working week, delivered remotely from a nearby time zone.

Companies in the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain often run several branches, warehouses or sales routes, and report to owners who want a clear picture every week. Many still rely on separate spreadsheets from each branch. GM builds one connected workbook so the numbers match and the owner sees the same view as the manager.

Our working hours overlap with yours. Pakistan time is one hour ahead of the UAE and two hours ahead of Saudi Arabia, so questions are answered the same working day and online meetings are easy to arrange.

What businesses in this region ask for

Branch and distributor sales dashboards

Compare branches, routes and salespeople against targets, with daily run-rate and projected month-end results.

Attendance for Friday and Saturday weekends

The HR tracker has a weekend setting for Friday and Saturday, so working days and attendance percentages are correct from the first day.

Stock and reorder tracking

Track stock in and out for shops and warehouses, see slow movers and products to reorder, and work out profit per product.

Seasonal peak planning

Plan and track targets for busy periods such as Ramadan and year-end sales with daily progress against the plan.

Owner and management packs

One monthly workbook with sales, costs, stock and staff numbers that fits on a single review meeting agenda.

Bilingual labels on request

Sheets can include labels in English and, on request, another language you provide. Please ask before ordering.

Why companies choose to work with GM

  • Close time zone. Pakistan is one to two hours ahead of the Gulf, so replies are quick.
  • Fits your week. Friday and Saturday weekends and local public holidays are built into the HR and attendance tools.
  • Clear pricing. Written fixed-price quotes in US dollars. Bank transfer is accepted.
  • No subscriptions. You own the Excel file and can use it offline.
  • Industry background. More than nine years of work in FMCG, distribution and consumer electronics reporting.

Questions

Which currency do you invoice in?

US dollars. Your bank converts to dirhams, riyals or other local currency. Please ask if you need a different arrangement.

Can you work in my branch's time zone?

Yes. Calls can be scheduled in your working hours. Most work is done asynchronously with written updates.

Do you handle VAT or tax reporting?

No. GM builds operational dashboards and trackers. Tax and accounting filing should stay with your accountant.

Can staff in several countries use the same workbook?

Yes. It is a normal Excel file. Share it the way you share other company files.

Request a quote

One short form. You get a written fixed-price quote, and you decide whether to go ahead.
