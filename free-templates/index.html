Free templates

Free Excel templates that really work

Lite versions of GM's most useful business templates. Free to download, with an easy upgrade if you outgrow them.

Four free templates, no sign-up

Download, open in Microsoft Excel (2010 or later) and use them straight away. Each has example data and a how-to sheet.

Attendance Tracker Lite

Log absences, leave, sick days, half days and late arrivals for up to 10 employees. See working days and attendance percentage for any month, with Saturday and Sunday, Friday and Saturday, or Sunday-only weekends.

  • Up to 10 employees
  • Monthly summary with attendance %
  • Weekend and holiday setting
Download free (.xlsx)See the full version

Team KPI Scorecard Lite

Score up to 5 people on up to 4 weighted KPIs. It handles higher-is-better and lower-is-better KPIs, caps scores at 120%, then gives a weighted score, a rating and a rank.

  • Up to 5 people, 4 KPIs
  • Weighted score and rating
  • Automatic ranking
Download free (.xlsx)See the full version

Monthly Budget Planner (50/30/20)

Enter your take-home income and your expenses, group each one as Needs, Wants or Savings, and see how your month compares with the 50/30/20 rule. It shows what is over target and how much money is left. Amounts are in rupees and the file comes with example numbers to replace.

  • Targets you can change
  • Planned and actual columns
  • Status for each group
Download free (.xlsx)Read the budget guide

Payroll & Salary Tax Lite

Work out monthly payroll and Pakistan income tax for up to 10 employees. Enter basic salary, allowances, bonus and provident fund and see gross pay, income tax, deductions and net pay for each person, using the 2026-27 salary tax slabs. It comes with example staff to replace.

  • Up to 10 employees
  • Tax year 2026-27 slabs built in
  • Quick tax check for any income
Download free (.xlsx)See the full version
