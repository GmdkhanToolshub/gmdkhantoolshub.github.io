GM Business Dashboard

Connection status and revenue tracking. Real data only.

Private owner page. It is hidden from search engines and the menu. It shows only real data: the status of your Google connections, and the orders and income you enter yourself. Nothing is estimated and no earnings are made up. Entries are saved only in this browser, so export a CSV regularly as a backup. Do not enter card numbers, PINs or passwords here.
