How to use the Sales Target Calculator

  1. Enter the sales target for the period and what has been achieved so far.
  2. Add the total selling days and how many are complete.
  3. Read the achievement percentage, remaining target and required daily sales.
  4. Compare the current run rate with the required rate to decide whether to adjust.

About this tool

Required daily sales = remaining target ÷ selling days left. The current run rate is achieved sales ÷ days completed. If the run rate is lower than the required rate, the projection falls short. Sales teams use the gap between the two to decide where to focus in the remaining days.

Worked example: mid-month check

A team has a target of Rs 5,000,000 for a month with 26 selling days. After 15 days, it has sold Rs 3,200,000.

The team's run rate is above the rate it needs, so it's on track. If the run rate were lower than the required rate, the gap would show exactly how much more it needs to sell each day.

Using run rate for daily decisions

  • Check it often: a gap is easier to close on day 10 than on day 25.
  • Use true selling days: leave out weekly closures and public holidays, or the daily requirement will look too low.
  • Break it down: run the numbers by region, team or salesperson to see where the gap sits.
  • Compare two rates: if the required rate is rising each day, the target is slipping away.

Limits of a straight-line projection

The projection assumes the rest of the period will look like the days so far. Real sales often don't move evenly. Month-end pushes, promotions, payday patterns, seasons and stock shortages can all change the result, so treat the projection as an early signal and not a promise.

Frequently asked questions

Should I count weekends as selling days?

Only if your team sells on those days. Use the number of working or selling days that actually apply to your route or channel.

Why does the projection differ from the required daily sales?

The projection assumes you keep your current daily average. The required figure is what you must average from now on to hit the target.

Can I use it for any currency or unit?

Yes. It works with any numbers, such as revenue, cases or units. The optional currency label is just text.

How do I calculate target achievement percentage?

Divide achieved sales by the target and multiply by 100. Selling Rs 3,200,000 against a Rs 5,000,000 target is 64%.

How do I calculate required daily sales?

Divide your remaining target by the selling days left. Rs 1,800,000 over 11 days is about Rs 163,636 a day.

What is a sales run rate?

It is your average sales per selling day so far. Multiply it by the total selling days to project where the period may finish.
