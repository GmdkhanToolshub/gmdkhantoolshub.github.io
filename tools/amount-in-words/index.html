How to use the Amount in Words Converter (Lakh & Crore)

  1. Type the amount in figures. You can use commas and up to two decimal places.
  2. Choose the currency, the number system and the style you want.
  3. Press Copy words, then check the words against your figures before you use them.

About this tool

Cheques, vouchers and legal documents need the amount written in words as well as figures. This tool turns a number into English words using either the Pakistani and Indian system (thousand, lakh, crore, arab, kharab) or the international system (thousand, million, billion). It works up to 15 digits and runs entirely in your browser.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are estimates for general information; see the disclaimer.

Example

125000 is written Rupees One Lakh Twenty-Five Thousand Only. In the international system the same number is "One Hundred Twenty-Five Thousand". Two lakh fifty thousand and 50 paisa, 250050.50, becomes "Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Fifty and Fifty Paisa Only".

Tips for writing cheques

  • Write "Only" after the words and draw a line through any empty space so nothing can be added.
  • The amount in words and in figures must match. If they differ, the bank may return the cheque.
  • Start the words at the far left of the line and avoid corrections. Use a new cheque if you make a mistake.
  • Check Roman Urdu text with the Roman Urdu to Urdu tool, or type Urdu directly with the Urdu Keyboard.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write 1,00,000 in words?

It is One Lakh. In the international system it is One Hundred Thousand.

How do I write 1 crore in words?

1,00,00,000 is One Crore, which is Ten Million in the international system.

Does it handle paisa?

Yes. Type up to two decimal places and the paisa are written after the rupees, for example "and Fifty Paisa".

Can it write amounts in Urdu?

This version writes English words only. Many cheques and accounts use English words, but check what your bank accepts.

How large a number can it write?

Up to 15 digits, which covers amounts into the hundreds of kharab.
