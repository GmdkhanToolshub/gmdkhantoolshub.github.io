How to use the Digital Clock

  1. Choose a time zone, or keep your device's own.
  2. Switch 12 or 24 hour format, seconds and the date.
  3. Pick a colour style.
  4. Press Full screen for a clock you can see from across the room.

About this tool

The clock reads your device's own clock and formats it for the time zone you choose using your browser's built-in time zone data, including daylight saving changes.

Good to know: The time is only as accurate as your device's clock. Full screen is not available in every mobile browser.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are for general information; see the disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

Is the time accurate?

It matches your device's clock. If your device is set to update its time automatically, it will be very accurate.

Can I show another city's time?

Yes. Choose a time zone from the list. For several cities at once, use the World Clock.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded it keeps running without an internet connection.

A full-screen clock for study, work and events

A large digital clock is handy on a spare screen during exams, meetings, workshops or study sessions. Seeing the time at a glance helps you stay on schedule without picking up your phone.

Open it in a separate tab or on a second device and leave it running. It uses your device clock, so it follows the time zone set on that device.

Practical tips

  • Use full screen on a tablet or old laptop as a desk clock.
  • Pair it with the Pomodoro Timer for study blocks.
  • If the time looks wrong, check your device date and time settings.
  • Keep the screen brightness low in a dark room.

Worked example

A team in Lahore has a call with a client in London at 3:00 pm London time. Choose the London time zone on the clock, switch to 24 hour format, and note the time. In November, London is five hours behind Lahore, so 15:00 in London is 20:00 in Lahore. Press Full screen to keep a large clock on a spare monitor during the meeting.
