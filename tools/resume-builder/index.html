How to use the Professional Resume Builder

  1. Choose a template and accent colour.
  2. Enter your contact details, summary, experience, education and skills.
  3. Check the live preview on the right.
  4. Download the PDF or a plain-text version.

About this tool

The PDF uses real selectable text and a simple single-column layout with standard section headings, which is what most applicant tracking systems read most reliably. Your data is saved in your own browser so you can come back to it. It is never uploaded.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are estimates for general information; see the disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

Will recruiters and ATS software read this PDF?

The PDF contains real text rather than images, with standard headings and no tables, which is the layout most systems parse best.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser's local storage on this device. Clear it any time with “Clear all”.

Can I use accented or non-Latin characters?

The PDF export supports Western European characters. Other scripts will be replaced with a question mark, so use the .txt download or print the preview for those.
