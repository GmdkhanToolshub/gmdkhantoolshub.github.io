How to use the UK Take-Home Pay Calculator 2026-27

  1. Enter your gross pay and say whether it is a yearly or monthly figure.
  2. Optionally enter the percentage you pay into a workplace pension (taken before tax).
  3. Read your tax, National Insurance and take-home pay for the year, month and week.

About this tool

Rates used for the 2026-27 tax year: Personal Allowance 12,570 pounds, reduced by 1 pound for every 2 pounds of income over 100,000. Taxable income is taxed at 20% up to 37,700 pounds, 40% up to 125,140 pounds and 45% above that. Employee National Insurance is 8% on earnings between 12,570 and 50,270 pounds and 2% above. A workplace pension is treated as a net-pay arrangement, which lowers taxable pay but not National Insurance. Scottish rates, student loans, the marriage allowance, benefits in kind and tax codes are not included. Check GOV.UK for official figures.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are estimates for general information; see the disclaimer.

National Insurance for employees is 8% on earnings between 12,570 and 50,270 pounds a year and 2% on earnings above 50,270. Between 100,000 and 125,140 pounds the Personal Allowance is withdrawn, which makes the effective rate on that slice 60%.

Example

On a salary of 35,000 pounds, taxable income is 35,000 minus 12,570 = 22,430. Tax at 20% is 4,486. National Insurance at 8% on 22,430 is 1,794. Take-home pay is about 28,720 pounds a year, or about 2,393 a month.

Ways to keep more of your pay

  • Pension contributions reduce your taxable pay.
  • Check your tax code on your payslip. A wrong code can mean overpaid tax.
  • If you send money home, compare services with the Send Money to Pakistan Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much tax do I pay on 30,000 pounds in the UK?

For 2026-27 the income tax on 30,000 is 3,486 and National Insurance is about 1,394, leaving about 25,120 a year before any pension or student loan.

Does this include Scotland?

No. Scotland sets its own income tax rates and bands. This calculator covers England, Wales and Northern Ireland.

Are student loans and benefits included?

No. Student loan repayments, benefits in kind, the marriage allowance and unusual tax codes are not included.

What does the pension box do?

It treats your contribution as a net-pay arrangement. The amount comes off your pay before income tax, so you pay less tax, while National Insurance is still charged on the full pay.

Are these the official figures?

They follow the published 2026-27 rates, but the calculator is an estimate. Confirm with GOV.UK or your payslip.
