How to use the Amount in Words & Urdu Number Converter

  1. Choose a tab: Amount in words, or Convert digits.
  2. For amounts, enter the number and choose lakh/crore or million/billion, and the currency words.
  3. Copy the words onto your cheque, invoice or receipt.
  4. To convert digits, paste text and choose the script you want.

About this tool

Amounts are written in the South Asian system (thousand, lakh, crore, arab) or the international system (thousand, million, billion, trillion), with rupees and paisa, dollars and cents, or no currency. Everything is calculated in your browser.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. See the privacy policy and disclaimer.

How to write an amount in words for a cheque

Most cheques in Pakistan ask for the amount in both figures and words. Enter the number above with "Rupees and paisa" selected, and you'll get wording like this:

AmountIn words
1,25,000Rupees One Lakh Twenty-Five Thousand Only
15,50,000Rupees Fifteen Lakh Fifty Thousand Only
1,00,00,000Rupees One Crore Only
2,500.75Rupees Two Thousand Five Hundred and Seventy-Five Paisa Only

Adding "Only" at the end stops anyone from adding extra words. Always check the amount carefully before signing.

Lakh and crore vs million and billion

South AsianNumberInternational
1 lakh1,00,000100 thousand
10 lakh10,00,0001 million
1 crore1,00,00,00010 million
1 arab1,00,00,00,0001 billion

Notice the comma pattern: South Asian numbers group the last three digits, then every two digits (1,25,000), while international numbers group every three (125,000).

Urdu, Arabic and Hindi digits

The "Convert digits" tab changes every number in your text to the script you choose: English 0123456789, Urdu ۰۱۲۳۴۵۶۷۸۹, Arabic ٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩ or Hindi ०१२३४५६७८९. It's useful for Urdu documents, invitations, school work and signs. To type the text itself in Urdu, use the Urdu Keyboard.

Frequently asked questions

How do I write 1,25,000 in words?

One lakh twenty-five thousand. On a cheque, write: Rupees One Lakh Twenty-Five Thousand Only.

How many lakh are in a crore?

One crore equals 100 lakh, which is 10 million.

What is the difference between Urdu and Arabic digits?

They look similar, but the Urdu (Persian) digits for 4, 5 and 6 are written differently from the Arabic ones. This tool converts between both.
