How to use the Salary Calculator

  1. Enter your salary and choose whether it is hourly, monthly, annual or another period.
  2. Pick your currency and check the working hours, days and paid weeks that apply to your job.
  3. Optionally add a flat tax or deduction percentage and an annual bonus.
  4. Read the results table, which shows gross and estimated net pay for every period.

About this tool

The calculator first converts your pay to an annual figure using your working hours and paid weeks, adds any bonus, and then divides back down to each period. The tax field applies one flat percentage, so it is a quick estimate rather than a payroll calculation. Real tax depends on your country, allowances, brackets and benefits.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are estimates for general information; see the disclaimer.

How salary conversion works

Every pay figure, whether hourly, daily or monthly, is a slice of the same yearly total. The calculator turns what you enter into an annual amount, adds any bonus, and then divides that total back down into each period.

Gross pay and take-home pay

Gross pay is your agreed salary before anything is taken out. Net pay is what reaches your bank account. The gap usually comes from income tax, plus deductions such as a provident fund, social security or health contributions, or loan repayments. Check your payslip to see which ones apply to you.

The calculator uses one flat percentage for all of these. To find a realistic figure, add up the deductions on a recent payslip and divide by your gross pay. If Rs 12,000 comes out of Rs 150,000, enter 8%.

Turning the slabs into a percentage for the calculator

Slab rates apply only to the part of your salary inside each band, so your overall rate is lower than the band you fall in. To estimate your overall rate, assuming your whole salary is taxable:

  1. Work out your annual salary. Rs 150,000 × 12 = Rs 1,800,000.
  2. Apply your band. Rs 1,800,000 falls in the 11% band, so tax = 6,000 + 11% × (1,800,000 − 1,200,000) = Rs 72,000.
  3. Divide tax by salary. 72,000 ÷ 1,800,000 = 4%. Enter 4 in the tax field.

Comparing two job offers

A higher monthly salary is not always the better offer, so compare annual totals. Suppose Offer A pays Rs 180,000 a month with no bonus, and Offer B pays Rs 165,000 a month plus a two-month bonus of Rs 330,000:

  • Offer A: 180,000 × 12 = Rs 2,160,000 a year.
  • Offer B: (165,000 × 12) + 330,000 = Rs 2,310,000 a year, which is Rs 150,000 more.

Then check how each offer is really paid. Ask whether the bonus is guaranteed or depends on performance, because a discretionary bonus may not arrive. Also compare working hours, since a longer week lowers your effective hourly rate, and benefits such as medical cover, a provident fund or a car allowance.

Getting more accurate results

  • Paid weeks: keep 52 if you are paid through holidays and leave. Lower it for contract work with unpaid breaks.
  • Bonus: the amount is spread across every period, so the monthly figure includes one-twelfth of it. Leave it at zero to see your regular pay alone.
  • Currency: the selector changes the currency label only. To compare pay in another currency, use the Currency Converter.
  • Raises: use the Percentage Calculator to work out the percentage difference between your current and proposed salary.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an hourly wage to an annual salary?

Multiply the hourly rate by hours per week and by paid weeks per year. For example, 20 per hour × 40 hours × 52 weeks = 41,600 per year.

Does the calculator work out my exact tax?

No. It applies a single flat percentage that you enter. Progressive tax systems, allowances and social contributions vary by country, so use it only as a planning estimate.

Which currencies are supported?

Twenty common currencies are available for formatting. The tool does not convert between currencies; use the Currency Converter for that.

How much tax is deducted from a Rs 150,000 monthly salary in Pakistan?

Using the reported rates for tax year 2027, and assuming the whole salary is taxable, annual tax is Rs 72,000. That is Rs 6,000 a month, or 4% of gross pay. Other deductions are extra, and your payroll figure may differ.

How do I calculate an hourly rate from a monthly salary?

Multiply the monthly salary by 12, then divide by paid weeks per year and by hours per week. For example, Rs 150,000 × 12 ÷ 52 ÷ 40 is about Rs 865 an hour.

Why does the calculator use a flat tax rate?

Tax rules differ by country and change often, so the tool lets you enter your own overall percentage. To find it, divide your total annual deductions by your annual gross pay.
