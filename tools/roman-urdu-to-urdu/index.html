How to use the Roman Urdu to Urdu Converter

  1. Type or paste Roman Urdu in the first box.
  2. The Urdu script appears as you type.
  3. Check any words marked as spelled by sound, and correct them in the Urdu box if needed.
  4. Copy the Urdu text or download it as a text file.

About this tool

The converter first looks up each word in a dictionary of common Urdu words and phrases, including greetings like Assalam o Alaikum and InshaAllah. Words it doesn't know are spelled by sound using Urdu spelling rules. Everything happens in your browser, so your text is never uploaded.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. See the privacy policy and disclaimer.

What is Roman Urdu?

Roman Urdu is Urdu written with English letters, as most people do in WhatsApp messages, comments and texts. It's quick to type, but for letters, posts, invitations, school work and official notices you often need proper Urdu script. This tool converts it for you, with no Urdu keyboard needed.

Tips for the best results

  • Use common spellings: "hai", "nahi" and "bohat" are all recognised, along with many variations.
  • Use capital letters for hard sounds: type a capital T, D or R for ٹ، ڈ and ڑ, for example "beTa" for بیٹا or "baRa" for بڑا.
  • Check words spelled by sound: Roman Urdu has no fixed spelling, so the tool tells you how many words it guessed. Correct any mistakes directly in the Urdu box.
  • Keep punctuation: question marks, commas and full stops become ؟، and ۔ automatically.

Private and free

Many online converters send your text to a server. This one works entirely in your browser, so personal messages stay on your device. To type directly in Urdu instead, try the Urdu Keyboard.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Roman Urdu converter free?

Yes. It is free with no sign-up, and your text is converted in your browser without being uploaded.

Why is a word spelled wrongly?

Roman Urdu has no standard spelling, so words that aren't in the dictionary are spelled by sound. You can correct them directly in the Urdu box.

How do I type ٹ, ڈ or ڑ?

Use a capital T, D or R, for example 'beTa' for بیٹا and 'baRa' for بڑا.

Can I use the Urdu text in Word or WhatsApp?

Yes. Copy it and paste it anywhere that supports Urdu, including Word, Google Docs, WhatsApp and Facebook.
