How to use the PDF Merge, Split & Extract

  1. Choose Merge or Split at the top of the tool.
  2. Drop your PDF files in, or click to choose them.
  3. For merging, put the files in order; for splitting, type the pages or choose how to split.
  4. Download your new PDF, or a ZIP of split files.

About this tool

This tool uses pdf-lib, an open-source PDF library, inside your browser. Pages are copied exactly, so text stays selectable and quality is unchanged. Password-protected PDFs need the password removed first.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are for general information; see the disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Merging and splitting happen in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to our servers.

Is there a page or file limit?

Each file can be up to 100 MB. Very large documents depend on your device's memory, so close other tabs if a big merge is slow.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

Not directly. Open the PDF with its password, save a copy without the password, then merge that copy.

How your data is handled

This tool runs in your browser. What you enter is processed on your device and not uploaded to our servers.

Worked example

You have a CV, a cover letter and a degree scan as separate files. Choose Merge, drop all three in, put them in the order the employer wants, and download one combined PDF. Later, to send only pages 2 to 4 of a long report, choose Split, type 2-4 and download just those pages.

Practical tips

  • Put files in the right order before you merge.
  • Use page ranges like 2-4 or 1,3,5 to extract pages.
  • Remove a password first if a PDF is protected.
  • Check the page count of the result before sending.
