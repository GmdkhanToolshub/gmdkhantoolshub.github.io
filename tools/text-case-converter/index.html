About this tool

Title Case keeps short words such as 'and', 'of' and 'the' in lower case unless they start the text. Developer styles such as camelCase and snake_case work line by line, so you can convert a list of names at once.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are for general information; see the disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between title case and sentence case?

Title case capitalises most words, like a headline. Sentence case capitalises only the first word of each sentence, like normal writing.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Press Undo to step back through your last 30 changes.

Is my text stored?

No. It is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to our servers.
