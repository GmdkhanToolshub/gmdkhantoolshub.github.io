Fuel Cost & Mileage Calculator

Work out how much petrol or diesel a trip needs, what it costs, your cost per kilometre and your monthly commute bill, and split the cost between the people riding.

How to use the Fuel Cost & Mileage Calculator

  1. Enter the one-way distance, your mileage in km per litre and today's fuel price.
  2. Tick round trip, add tolls or parking, and set how many trips you make in a month.
  3. Enter the number of people if you share the cost, then read the totals.

About this tool

Fuel cost is distance divided by mileage (litres used) times the price per litre. This calculator adds tolls and parking, scales the answer to a month and a year, and divides it between the people sharing the ride. It does not know today's pump price, so you type it in. Everything is calculated in your browser.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are estimates for general information; see the disclaimer.

The fuel cost formula

Cost = distance ÷ mileage × price per litre. A 25 km trip each way is 50 km for the round trip. At 12 km per litre that needs 4.17 litres. At an example price of Rs 300 per litre the fuel costs about Rs 1,250. Over 26 working days that comes to Rs 32,500 a month.

Typical mileage

VehicleTypical km per litre
Motorbike (70 to 125 cc)35 to 55
Small car (660 to 1000 cc)14 to 20
Sedan (1300 to 1600 cc)10 to 15
SUV or 4×46 to 10

These are ranges, not promises. Your own average is best: fill the tank, note the odometer, drive normally, refill and divide the kilometres by the litres added.

How to spend less on fuel

  • Keep tyres at the right pressure and avoid carrying extra weight.
  • Drive smoothly. Hard acceleration and braking waste fuel, and high speed uses more.
  • Use the AC sensibly in slow traffic, and service the engine on time.
  • Share the ride. Enter the number of people to see each person's share.
  • Compare car and bike costs, or check what you save when your salary rises, with the Salary Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate fuel cost for a trip?

Divide the distance by your mileage in km per litre to get litres, then multiply by the price per litre. 100 km at 10 km/l and Rs 300/l costs Rs 3,000.

How do I find my car's real mileage?

Fill the tank, note the odometer, drive as usual, then refill. Divide the kilometres driven by the litres you added.

Can I enter miles?

Yes. Choose Miles for the distance. Mileage stays in km per litre, so convert it if your figure is in miles per gallon.

Does it include tolls and parking?

Yes, there is a box for tolls and parking per trip that is added to the fuel cost.

Does it know today's petrol price?

No. Prices change, so you type the current pump price.

Worked example

A trip is 120 km and your car does 12 km per litre. Fuel needed is 120 divided by 12, which is 10 litres. At 270 rupees a litre, the fuel costs 2,700 rupees. A return journey doubles it to 5,400. Add tolls or parking if the tool asks, and split the total by the number of passengers to share costs fairly.

Practical tips

  • Use your real average from a full tank, not the maker's claim.
  • Remember to double the distance for a return trip.
  • Update the fuel price, as it changes often.
  • Split the total by passengers to share costs.
