About these tools and gadgets

Everything here is free and runs in your browser. The PDFs, images, documents and text you work with are processed on your own device with open-source libraries, and are not uploaded to our servers. There is nothing to install and no account to make. Star a tool to keep it in your favourites, and tools you use recently appear at the top next time.

What these converters cannot do

The PDF to Word and PDF to Excel tools read the real text inside a PDF. A scanned PDF is only a picture of a page, so converting it needs OCR (text recognition), which a free browser tool cannot do reliably. For scans, use PDF to JPG to get clean page images. Word to PDF and Excel to PDF reproduce text, lists, tables and values, but not every font, chart or special layout, and they cannot draw letters outside the Latin alphabet such as Urdu or Arabic. Each tool page lists its own limits clearly before you start.
