How to use the Zakat Calculator

  1. Enter today's gold and silver prices per tola or per gram.
  2. Add the gold and silver you own, plus cash, savings, stock and money owed to you.
  3. Deduct debts and bills that are due now.
  4. Choose the silver or gold nisab and see your zakat.

About this tool

Nisab is 52.5 tola (612.36 g) of silver or 7.5 tola (87.48 g) of gold. Many scholars recommend the silver nisab because it is lower, so more people give. One tola is taken as 11.6638 grams.

Frequently asked questions

Which nisab should I use, gold or silver?

Many scholars advise the silver nisab because it benefits more people in need. Follow the guidance of the scholar you trust.

Does the calculator know today's gold price?

No. Enter the current local price yourself, for example from a jeweller or a gold rate published that day. This keeps the tool free and private.

Is this a religious ruling?

No. It applies the common 2.5% calculation. Rulings on jewellery, pensions, property and debts differ, so confirm your situation with a qualified scholar.

Roman Urdu mein aam sawalat

Zakat kitne percent hoti hai?

Zakat qabil-e-zakat maal ka 2.5 percent hoti hai, jab maal nisab tak pohanch jaye aur us par ek qamri saal guzar chuka ho.

Nisab kya hota hai?

Sone ka nisab saade saat tola (taqreeban 87.48 gram) aur chandi ka saade baawan tola mana jata hai. Aap kaun sa nisab istemal karein, is ke liye kisi mustanad aalim se poochein.

Kya yeh calculator fatwa deta hai?

Nahin. Yeh sirf hisaab mein madad ke liye hai. Shari masail ke liye kisi mustanad aalim ya mufti se rujoo karein.

Worked example

Suppose you own 5 tola of gold, valued at 400,000 rupees per tola, so 2,000,000, plus 300,000 in cash, and owe 100,000 in bills that are due. Net wealth is 2,200,000. If silver is 5,000 rupees per tola, the silver nisab of 52.5 tola is 262,500, so you are above it. Zakat is 2.5% of 2,200,000, which is 55,000 rupees. Use today's prices, not these examples.

Practical tips

  • Use current gold and silver prices on your zakat date.
  • Deduct only debts that are due now.
  • Many scholars use the silver nisab because it is lower.
  • Ask a qualified scholar about unusual assets or cases.
