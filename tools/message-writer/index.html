How to use the Email & WhatsApp Message Writer

  1. Choose Email or WhatsApp and the situation.
  2. Enter the recipient, your name, the topic and any key details or deadline.
  3. Pick a tone and press Write message.
  4. Edit the draft, then copy it or open it in your email or WhatsApp app.

About this tool

Drafts are built from tested business-writing patterns: a clear purpose first, the supporting detail second, and a specific request or next step last. WhatsApp messages are shorter and more conversational. Always review before sending, especially names, dates and amounts.

Email or WhatsApp?

  • Email is better for formal requests, anything you may need as a record, and messages with attachments. The tool adds a subject line and a sign-off.
  • WhatsApp is common in Pakistan for quick business contact. The tool writes a shorter message and can open it directly in WhatsApp.

For payment reminders or leave requests, send an email even if you also message on WhatsApp, so there's a written record.

Picking the right tone

  • Formal: for senior managers, new clients and official requests.
  • Friendly: for colleagues and clients you know well.
  • Short and direct: for busy readers and quick updates.

Example: a polite payment reminder

Dear Sara,

This is a friendly reminder regarding payment for invoice INV-104.

The amount due is Rs 85,000.

Please arrange payment by Friday 13 November, or let me know if there is an issue.

If you have already paid, kindly ignore this message.

Kind regards,
Ali

A reminder like this stays polite while asking for a specific action by a specific date.

Tips for messages that get replies

  1. Put the request near the top: busy people may not read to the end.
  2. Ask one clear question: one specific question gets a faster answer than several vague ones.
  3. Include a date: "by Thursday" creates a clear next step.
  4. Keep it short: on WhatsApp, a few lines is usually enough.
  5. Check the draft: confirm the names, amounts and dates before you send it.

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool send messages for me?

No. It opens your own email or WhatsApp app with the text prefilled, and you decide whether to send.

Can I use it for formal and informal tone?

Yes. Choose Formal, Friendly or Short & direct.

Is my text stored?

No. Drafts are generated in your browser.

Can I send the message straight to WhatsApp?

Yes. Choose WhatsApp as the channel, then use Open in WhatsApp to open the message, ready for you to pick a contact and send.

How do I write a polite payment reminder?

Choose Payment reminder, add the invoice or topic and the amount, and give a date. The draft asks for payment politely and covers the case where it has already been paid.

Is this message writer free?

Yes. It's free, with no sign-up, and your text stays in your browser.
