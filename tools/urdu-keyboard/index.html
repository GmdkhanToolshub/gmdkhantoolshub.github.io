How to use the Urdu Keyboard

  1. Click in the text box and start typing. Each English key types the Urdu letter that sounds like it.
  2. Hold Shift for extra letters, or tick 'Show Shift letters' to see them on screen.
  3. Tap the on-screen keys if you're on a phone or tablet.
  4. Copy your text or download it as a text file.

About this tool

The keyboard uses a phonetic layout, so a gives ا, b gives ب, s gives س, and so on. It's the easiest way to learn because the keys match the sounds. Your text stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

Punctuation also changes automatically: comma, full stop and question mark become ، ۔ and ؟. Numbers can be typed as Urdu digits (۱۲۳) or English digits (123). Untick "Urdu digits" for English numbers.

Typing tips

  • Do-chashmi he (ھ): type Shift+H after a letter for sounds like بھ، پھ، کھ, for example "bH" gives بھ.
  • Noon ghunna (ں): use Shift+N at the end of words like ہیں and میں.
  • Switch to English: untick "Urdu typing on" to type English letters in the same box.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install an Urdu keyboard?

No. This keyboard works in your browser on any computer, tablet or phone, with nothing to install.

How do I type آ, ں and ٹ?

Hold Shift: Shift+A gives آ, Shift+N gives ں and Shift+T gives ٹ. Tick 'Show Shift letters' to see them on screen.

Is my text saved anywhere?

No. Your text stays in your browser and is cleared when you close the page, unless you copy or download it.
