PDF to Word

How to use the PDF to Word

  1. Drop a text-based PDF into the box, or click to choose one.
  2. Choose whether to detect headings and whether each PDF page starts a new Word page.
  3. Press Convert to Word.
  4. Download the .docx file and open it in Word, Google Docs or LibreOffice.

About this tool

The tool reads the text layer of your PDF with pdf.js, groups it into lines and paragraphs, treats noticeably larger text as headings, and builds a standard .docx file. The result opens in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice and WPS Office.

Good to know: This is a text conversion, not an exact copy. Images, tables, columns, headers, footers and fonts are not reproduced, and you may need to tidy some lines in Word. Scanned PDFs are pictures of text, so they cannot be converted without OCR (text recognition), which is not included. Right-to-left languages such as Urdu and Arabic may come out in a different order.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are for general information; see the disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

Will the layout stay exactly the same?

No. You get the text, headings and paragraphs, but not the original page layout, pictures or columns. For an exact visual copy, use PDF to JPG instead.

Why does my scanned PDF say there is no text?

A scan is a photograph of a page, so it contains no real text to extract. Turning it into text needs OCR, which this free browser tool does not include.

Can I edit the result in Google Docs?

Yes. Upload the .docx file to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs.

Worked example

A text-based report arrives as a PDF and you need to edit it. Drop it in, keep heading detection on, and press Convert to Word. Open the .docx in Word or Google Docs: headings and paragraphs are editable, though complex tables or columns may need tidying. Compare it with the PDF side by side before you rely on it.

Practical tips

  • Works best on text-based PDFs, not scans.
  • Expect to re-fit tables and multi-column layouts.
  • Turn heading detection on for reports and off for plain letters.
  • Always compare the result with the original.
