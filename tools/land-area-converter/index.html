How to use the Marla to Square Feet Converter

  1. Choose Convert area, type a number and pick its unit. Every other unit appears at once.
  2. Or choose Plot size, enter the length and width, and optionally a price per marla.
  3. Pick the marla size that applies to you. Standard is 272.25 sq ft; some housing schemes use 225 sq ft.

About this tool

Land in Pakistan is sold in marla and kanal, but the exact size of a marla is not the same everywhere. The standard marla is 272.25 square feet (30.25 square yards), the figure used for revenue records in Punjab. Some housing societies measure a marla as 225 square feet. This converter lets you pick either one. Everything is calculated in your browser.

How to calculate plot size

Multiply length by width to get square feet, then divide by 272.25 to get marla. A 30 ft by 60 ft plot is 1,800 sq ft, which is 6.61 marla. Divide the marla by 20 to get kanal. For plots measured in yards or metres, the plot tab converts them first.

Things to check before you buy

  • Which marla? A "5 marla" plot in a scheme with 225 sq ft marla is smaller than 5 standard marla. Ask which size the file or society uses.
  • Documents: measured area on the ground can differ from the area written in the fard, registry or allotment letter. Check the paperwork and have the plot measured.
  • Price per marla: enter it in the plot tab to see the total, then compare plots of different sizes on the same basis.
  • Financing: if you plan to borrow for a house or plot, estimate repayments with the Loan & EMI Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet are in one marla?

A standard marla is 272.25 square feet. Some housing schemes use 225 square feet, so the tool lets you choose.

How many marla make one kanal?

One kanal is 20 marla, which is 5,445 square feet at the standard marla size.

How many marla are in an acre?

An acre is 43,560 square feet, which is 160 standard marla or 8 kanal.

How do I find the marla size of a plot from its length and width?

Multiply length by width in feet and divide by 272.25. For example, 30 × 60 ft = 1,800 sq ft = about 6.61 marla.

Why does my plot differ from its named size?

Plots are often a little bigger or smaller than their marketing size. The registry or fard shows the exact area.

Worked example

A plot is 5 marla. With a marla of 272.25 square feet, that is 1,361.25 square feet. One kanal is 20 marla, so 5,445 square feet. If your housing society uses a 225 square foot marla, the same 5 marla is 1,125 square feet, which shows why you must check the marla size on the file before you agree a price.

Practical tips

  • Confirm the marla size your society or revenue record uses.
  • Remember that 1 kanal equals 20 marla.
  • Convert to square feet when comparing prices from different areas.
  • Use the same unit on both sides when checking a plot map against a file.
