How to use the Color Picker & Converter

  1. Click the colour swatch to pick a colour, or type a value in any format.
  2. All other formats update at once. Press Copy next to the one you need.
  3. Use Pick from an image or Pick from screen to take a colour from anywhere.
  4. Try the palette ideas and save colours you like.

About this tool

Conversions use standard formulas, and the tool also shows how readable white or black text would be on your colour. CMYK here is a simple mathematical conversion, not a print-profile match.

Good to know: CMYK values are approximate because real print colours depend on the printer and paper. The screen eyedropper needs a recent Chrome or Edge. Saved colours stay in this browser.

Free to use. Your inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to our servers. Results are for general information; see the disclaimer.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert HEX to RGB?

Type or paste the HEX code, such as #1D5BD8. The RGB value appears instantly, and you can copy it.

Why do the CMYK values look different from my printer?

CMYK here is a simple calculation. Printers use their own colour profiles, so printed colours differ a little.

Can I pick a colour from a photo?

Yes. Choose Pick from an image, open your picture and click anywhere on it.

How your data is handled

This tool runs in your browser. What you enter is processed on your device and not uploaded to our servers.

Worked example

Enter the HEX colour #1E90FF. The tool shows RGB 30, 144, 255, HSL 210 degrees, 100% saturation and 56% lightness, and the matching HSV and CMYK values. Copy the format your project needs, for example the HEX for CSS or the RGB for a slide. Use Pick from an image to take the exact blue from your logo instead of guessing.

Practical tips

  • Copy HEX for websites and RGB for screen design tools.
  • CMYK here is a mathematical estimate, so confirm print colours with your printer.
  • Pick from an image to match a logo exactly.
  • Check text readability on your colour before using it as a background.
