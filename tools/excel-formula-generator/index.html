VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP or INDEX + MATCH?

  • XLOOKUP is the simplest and most flexible option, but it needs Excel 365 or Excel 2021 or later.
  • INDEX + MATCH works in all versions and can look to the left, which VLOOKUP can't.
  • VLOOKUP is widely known. Use exact match, or it can return a wrong answer when the list isn't sorted.

If your file will be shared with people on older versions, choose INDEX + MATCH.

How to use a generated formula

  1. Choose the task and enter your cell ranges exactly as they appear in your sheet, such as A2:A500.
  2. Copy the formula and paste it into the cell where you want the result.
  3. Before copying the formula down, press F4 to lock ranges that shouldn't move, turning A2:A500 into $A$2:$A$500.
  4. Check one result by hand to confirm it's right.

Fixing common Excel errors

ErrorUsual causeFix
#N/AThe lookup value wasn't foundCheck spelling and extra spaces, or wrap the formula in IFERROR
#VALUE!Text where a number is expectedConvert text numbers to real numbers
#REF!A referenced cell or column was deletedUpdate the ranges in the formula
#DIV/0!Division by zero or an empty cellCheck the inputs, or use IFERROR
#NAME?A function your Excel version doesn't have, or a typoUse an older alternative, such as INDEX + MATCH instead of XLOOKUP

A tip on lookups: numbers stored as text and invisible extra spaces are the most common reasons lookups fail. TRIM removes extra spaces.

Frequently asked questions

Does it work in Google Sheets?

Most formulas do. XLOOKUP, UNIQUE and TEXTJOIN are supported in Google Sheets, while DATEDIF behaves slightly differently in some locales.

Why do text criteria need quotes?

Excel treats unquoted words as names or cell references. Put text in straight double quotes, such as "North".

Should I use VLOOKUP or XLOOKUP?

XLOOKUP is more flexible and safer when columns change, but needs Excel 2021 or Microsoft 365. VLOOKUP and INDEX-MATCH work in every version.

Does this work in Google Sheets?

Most formulas, such as SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, VLOOKUP, INDEX and MATCH, work the same way in Google Sheets. Newer functions can differ by version, so test them once.

Which lookup formula should I use?

Use XLOOKUP if everyone has Excel 365 or 2021 or later. Otherwise, INDEX plus MATCH works in all versions and is more flexible than VLOOKUP.

Why does my VLOOKUP return #N/A?

Usually the lookup value isn't an exact match. Check for extra spaces, numbers stored as text, or the wrong lookup column.
