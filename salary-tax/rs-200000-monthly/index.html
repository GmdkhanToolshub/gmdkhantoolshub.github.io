Tax on Rs 200,000 monthly salary

Monthly income tax, annual tax and take-home pay for a Rs 200,000 salary in Pakistan, with the slab-by-slab working.

Answer for Rs 200,000 a month

Monthly taxRs 13,000
Annual taxRs 156,000
Take-home per monthRs 187,000
Effective rate6.5%

At Rs 2,400,000 a year this salary reaches the 20% band, so the last rupees you earn are taxed at 20%, while the overall (effective) rate is only 6.5%.

Of the Rs 200,000 you earn each month, Rs 13,000 goes in tax, which leaves Rs 187,000. Put another way, each Rs 1,000 of salary costs you about Rs 65.0 in tax on average.

How the tax is worked out

The first Rs 600,000 of your Rs 2,400,000 is tax-free, and 3 higher bands cover the rest. Each rate applies only to the income inside its band:

The bands add up to Rs 156,000 a year, or Rs 13,000 a month.

What a raise or a bonus does

A 10% raise to Rs 220,000 adds Rs 4,000 of tax each month, so you keep 80% of the extra Rs 20,000. One extra month of salary as a yearly bonus adds Rs 40,000 of tax, leaving you about Rs 160,000 of it.

Related and frequently asked questions

Use the Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator for any other amount, or the guide to income tax on salary for the full slab table and examples. All salary levels: salary tax by monthly salary.

How much tax is deducted from a Rs 200,000 monthly salary in Pakistan?

For 2026-27 the annual income tax on Rs 200,000 a month (Rs 2,400,000 a year) is Rs 156,000, which is Rs 13,000 a month, if the whole salary is taxable.

What is the take-home pay on Rs 200,000 a month?

About Rs 187,000 a month after income tax, before any other deductions such as provident fund or loan recovery.

What happens to my tax if I get one extra month of bonus?

With 13 salaries in the year your annual tax would be Rs 196,000 instead of Rs 156,000, so the bonus adds Rs 40,000 of tax and you keep about Rs 160,000 of it.
