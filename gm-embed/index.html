Frequently asked questions

What is GM Embed?

GM Embed lets you put selected GM Tools Hub calculators on your own website. You pick a widget, adjust the look, copy a line of code and paste it into your page. The widget runs from gmtoolshub.com inside a frame on your page.

Is GM Embed free?

The current widgets are free to embed on a website, with a small "GM Tools Hub" credit link that must stay visible. Paid plans (Pro and Agency) are planned for white-label and business features but are not available to buy yet.

Does the widget collect my visitors' data?

The calculators work in the visitor's browser. What a visitor types is not sent to GM Tools Hub servers, and the widget pages do not set cookies or load analytics. Your visitors' browsers still request the widget page from the hosting provider, as with any embedded content.

Which widgets can I embed?

Only widgets that passed the embed test are listed: Tola to Gram Converter & Gold Value, Zakat Calculator, Pakistan Salary Tax Calculator, Loan & EMI Calculator, Electricity Bill & Units Calculator, Fuel Cost & Mileage Calculator, Marla to Square Feet Converter, Amount in Words Converter (Lakh & Crore), Matric & FSc Marks Percentage Calculator, Percentage Calculator, Age Calculator, BMI Calculator, Profit Margin Calculator, Date Difference Calculator, Live Pakistan Rates. More are added only after they pass the same test.

Can I remove the GM Tools Hub credit?

Not on the free plan. Removing branding is planned for the Business plan and needs a licence checked by a server, which does not exist yet. Until then, the credit link stays on every widget.

Will it work in WordPress, Blogger, Wix or React?

The embed is a standard iframe or a one-line script, so it should work anywhere you can add custom HTML. It has been tested on a plain HTML page on a different domain. WordPress, Blogger and React instructions are provided but have not been tested on live sites yet.
