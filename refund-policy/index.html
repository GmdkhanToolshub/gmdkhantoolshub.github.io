Refund Policy

When refunds are available for paid GM HCM plans and how to request one. Effective when paid plans open.

Last updated 8 November 2026. This policy applies to paid GM HCM plans, which are still being prepared. Free tools and the demo cost nothing.

Try before you buy

Before paying, please use the free GM HCM demo and read the plan details so you can check that the product fits your needs.

When you can get a refund

  • First purchase: if you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund of your first subscription payment within 7 days of activation.
  • Duplicate or mistaken payments: if you paid twice, or paid the wrong amount, we will refund the difference after verifying the payments.
  • Service not delivered: if your plan was not activated after verified payment and we cannot fix this, we will refund the payment.

When refunds are not available

  • Renewals, unless a payment was made in error.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after the first activation.
  • Cases where the account was suspended for breaking the Terms & Conditions.

How to request a refund

Contact us through the contact page with your company name, the payment date and reference, and the reason. We reply within a few working days.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned using the original payment method where possible. For bank transfers, we will ask for the account details to refund to. Bank or gateway processing can take several working days, and any gateway fees that are not returned to us may be deducted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as GM HCM launches. The version in force when you paid applies to your purchase.
