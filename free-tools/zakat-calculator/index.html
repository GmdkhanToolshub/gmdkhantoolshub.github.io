Zakat Calculator

Calculate zakat at 2.5% on your zakatable wealth, using today's gold or silver price for the nisab. You enter the prices, so it works anywhere and offline.

How to use the Zakat Calculator

  1. Enter today's gold and silver prices per tola or per gram.
  2. Add the gold and silver you own, plus cash, savings, stock and money owed to you.
  3. Deduct debts and bills that are due now.
  4. Choose the silver or gold nisab and see your zakat.

About this tool

Nisab is 52.5 tola (612.36 g) of silver or 7.5 tola (87.48 g) of gold. Many scholars recommend the silver nisab because it is lower, so more people give. One tola is taken as 11.6638 grams.

Frequently asked questions

Which nisab should I use, gold or silver?

Many scholars advise the silver nisab because it benefits more people in need. Follow the guidance of the scholar you trust.

Does the calculator know today's gold price?

No. Enter the current local price yourself, for example from a jeweller or a gold rate published that day. This keeps the tool free and private.

Is this a religious ruling?

No. It applies the common 2.5% calculation. Rulings on jewellery, pensions, property and debts differ, so confirm your situation with a qualified scholar.
