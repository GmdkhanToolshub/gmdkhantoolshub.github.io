1,500 Square feet in Marla

1,500 square feet = 5.51 marla at the standard 272.25 sq ft marla. That is 0.2755 kanal and 166.67 square yards.

Marla5.51
Kanal0.2755
Square yards166.67
Square metres139.35

These figures use the common Punjab marla of 272.25 square feet. Some housing schemes use a 225 square feet marla, which makes 1,500 square feet equal to 6.67 marla. Check which one your documents use.

Before you buy or sell

  • Compare the area written in the registry or fard with the measured area.
  • Check which marla size the scheme uses before comparing plots.
  • Compare plots by price per square foot, not by their marketing names.

Frequently asked questions

How many marla is 1,500 square feet?

1,500 square feet is 5.51 marla at the standard marla of 272.25 square feet. At a 225 square feet marla it is 6.667 marla.

How many kanal is 1,500 square feet?

1,500 square feet is 0.2755 kanal (1 kanal = 5,445 square feet at the standard marla).

How many square yards is 1,500 square feet?

Divide by 9: 1,500 square feet is 166.67 square yards.

Which marla should I use?

Use the marla stated in your file or scheme documents. The common Punjab marla is 272.25 square feet, and some housing schemes use 225 square feet.
