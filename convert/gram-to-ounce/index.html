How to convert grams to ounces

Multiply the value in grams by 0.0353 to get ounces. Formula: Ounces = Grams × 0.0353. To go the other way, divide by 0.0353.

Where you will need grams to ounces

Food labels, spices, tea and postal packages in Pakistan use grams, while international recipes, couriers and scales may use ounces. One ounce is about 28.35 grams.

This page uses the ordinary ounce. Precious metals use the troy ounce of 31.1035 g, so check which one a gold or silver price means.
