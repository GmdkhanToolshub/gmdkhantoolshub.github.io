Zakat

Zakat nisab in tola and grams

The nisab is 7.5 tola of gold (87.48 g) or 52.5 tola of silver (612.36 g). Zakat is 2.5% of zakatable wealth above it, held for a lunar year.

How zakat is worked out

  1. Add up your zakatable wealth: cash, savings, gold and silver, trade goods and money owed to you that you expect back.
  2. Subtract debts that are due now.
  3. If what remains is at or above the nisab and you have held it for a lunar year, zakat is 2.5% of it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the nisab of zakat in tola?

The commonly stated nisab is 7.5 tola of gold or 52.5 tola of silver. In grams that is about 87.48 g of gold or 612.36 g of silver.

Should I use the gold or the silver nisab?

Scholars and schools differ. Many use the silver nisab for cash and savings because it is lower and more cautious. Ask a scholar you trust which applies to you.

How much zakat is due?

The usual rate is 2.5% (one fortieth) of zakatable wealth that has been held for a lunar year and is above the nisab, after deducting debts due now.

How do I find the value of the nisab today?

Multiply the nisab weight in tola by today's price per tola for gold or silver. Use the calculator on this page and type in the current local rate.

Does this page show today's gold or silver price?

No. Prices change through the day and differ by city, so you enter the current rate you trust.
