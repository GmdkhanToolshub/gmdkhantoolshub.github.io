750 Square feet in Marla

750 square feet = 2.75 marla at the standard 272.25 sq ft marla. That is 0.1377 kanal and 83.33 square yards.

Marla2.75
Kanal0.1377
Square yards83.33
Square metres69.68

These figures use the common Punjab marla of 272.25 square feet. Some housing schemes use a 225 square feet marla, which makes 750 square feet equal to 3.33 marla. Check which one your documents use.

Before you buy or sell

  • Compare the area written in the registry or fard with the measured area.
  • Check which marla size the scheme uses before comparing plots.
  • Compare plots by price per square foot, not by their marketing names.

Frequently asked questions

How many marla is 750 square feet?

750 square feet is 2.755 marla at the standard marla of 272.25 square feet. At a 225 square feet marla it is 3.333 marla.

How many kanal is 750 square feet?

750 square feet is 0.1377 kanal (1 kanal = 5,445 square feet at the standard marla).

How many square yards is 750 square feet?

Divide by 9: 750 square feet is 83.33 square yards.

Which marla should I use?

Use the marla stated in your file or scheme documents. The common Punjab marla is 272.25 square feet, and some housing schemes use 225 square feet.
