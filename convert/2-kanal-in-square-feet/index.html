2 Kanal in Square feet

2 kanal = 10,890 square feet at the standard 272.25 sq ft marla. That is 1,210 square yards, 1,011.71 square metres and 2 kanal.

Square feet10,890
Square yards1,210
Square metres1,011.71
Kanal2

These figures use the common Punjab marla of 272.25 square feet. Some housing schemes use a 225 square feet marla, which makes 2 kanal equal to 9,000 square feet. Always check which one your file uses.

Before you buy

  • Ask which marla the scheme uses: 272.25 or 225 square feet.
  • Compare the area in the registry or fard with the measured plot.
  • Compare plots on the same basis, such as price per square foot.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet is 2 kanal?

2 kanal is 10,890 square feet at the standard marla of 272.25 square feet. In housing schemes that use a 225 square feet marla it is 9,000 square feet.

How many square yards is 2 kanal?

2 kanal is 1,210 square yards (standard marla, 30.25 square yards per marla).

How many marla is 2 kanal?

2 kanal is 40 marla (1 kanal = 20 marla).

Is the area of my 2 kanal plot exactly 10,890 square feet?

Not always. Plots are often a little bigger or smaller than their named size, and some schemes use a smaller marla. The registry, fard or allotment letter shows the real area, so check it before you buy.
