How to convert kilograms to seer

Multiply the value in kilograms by 1.0717 to get seer. Formula: Seer = Kilograms × 1.0717. To go the other way, divide by 1.0717.

Where you will need kilograms to seer

The seer is a traditional South Asian weight. Many people, especially in markets and villages, still say seer when they talk about flour, ghee, sugar or vegetables, while the scale shows kilograms. This page converts between the two.

This converter uses the standard figures where a maund is 37.3242 kg and a seer is one fortieth of a maund, about 0.9331 kg. That means a kilogram is about 1.0717 seer.

Handy examples and checks

  • 5 kg is about 5.36 seer.
  • 10 kg is about 10.72 seer.
  • The old seer was not the same everywhere in history, so agree on which weight you mean when you trade.
  • For the larger unit, see Kilograms to Maund.
