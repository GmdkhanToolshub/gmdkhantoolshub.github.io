What decides the silver rate in Pakistan

Silver is priced worldwide in US dollars per troy ounce. Pakistani prices follow that international price, converted at the dollar rate, plus local premiums. When the dollar becomes more expensive in rupees, or when the international silver price rises, the price per tola rises too.

Pure and sterling silver

Pure (fine) silver is 99.9% silver and is used for bars and coins. Sterling silver is 92.5% silver and is common in jewellery and cutlery. Silver sold as ornaments in local markets can have a lower purity, so ask what you are paying for.

Buying or selling silver safely

  • Ask for the weight and the purity in writing on your receipt.
  • Compare more than one seller and ask for making charges separately.
  • When you sell, buyers usually deduct for purity and wastage, so the sale price is lower than the buying price.
  • Convert units with the Tola to Gram Converter. A tola is 11.6638 grams.

Silver and zakat

Zakat on silver depends on the weight you hold. The usual silver nisab is 52.5 tola (about 612.36 grams). The Zakat Calculator helps you work it out, and a qualified scholar can answer questions about your own case.

About this page

Silver prices come from the free gold-api.com feed and dollar rates from Exchange Rate API. A scheduled job on this site collects them once a day. The figures are estimates for general information, not financial advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as the silver (chandi) rate in the market?

No. This page shows an estimate from the international silver price converted to rupees. Local sellers add their own premium and may differ. Ask your jeweller for the exact rate.

How is this silver rate calculated?

The international price of one troy ounce of silver in US dollars is multiplied by the dollar to rupee rate, divided by 31.1035 grams in a troy ounce, and multiplied by 11.6638 grams in a tola.

How often is the rate updated?

Once a day by an automated job. For a live price, ask your jeweller.

What is the silver nisab for zakat?

The usual silver nisab is 52.5 tola (about 612.36 grams). The page shows its estimated value, and the Zakat Calculator helps with the full calculation. A qualified scholar can answer questions about your own case.

What is the difference between pure and sterling silver?

Pure (fine) silver is 99.9% silver. Sterling silver is 92.5% silver mixed with other metals, so it is worth a little less per gram.
